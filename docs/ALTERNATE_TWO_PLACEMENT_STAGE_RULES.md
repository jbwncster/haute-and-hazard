# Haute & Hazard — Alternate Stage Rules: Two Placements + Runner-Up Dragdagulan†

**Status: EXPERIMENTAL PLAYTEST MODULE — not part of the canonical v7.8 rules.**

Use this only when deliberately testing whether a two-placement Stage structure makes Stages feel more competitive and gives **Dragdagulan†** a stronger, more natural role in the game.

> **† Legal/IP review:** `Dragdagulan` is working prototype terminology credited to *Drag Den Philippines*. It remains subject to legal/IP, trademark, and publisher review before commercial release and may be renamed.

## Design question

The test asks:

> Does allowing each Stage to produce a Stage Winner and a Runner-Up — with the Runner-Up earning an optional Dragdagulan† challenge — create a stronger dramatic arc without making Stages too long or weakening the value of first place?

## Experimental Stage flow

### 1. Stage opens

Play the Stage normally. All standard v7.8 rules remain in effect unless this document changes them.

### 2. First Slay — Stage Winner

The first Queen who legally Slays the active Stage becomes the **Stage Winner**.

The Stage Winner:

- claims the Stage trophy;
- gains the Stage's normal printed Slay reward;
- resolves normal Slay effects;
- does **not** immediately trigger Curtain Call under this experimental module.

The Stage remains open until the closing procedure below is completed.

### 3. Finish the current round — Last Chance Runway

After the Stage Winner is determined, finish the current round so the remaining Queens have a fair chance to post a qualifying performance.

For this test, record each non-winning Queen's **highest Appeal reached during the Stage's closing round** before their normal Cleanup resets Appeal.

This recorded value exists only to determine Runner-Up. It does not become spendable Appeal and does not carry into later turns.

### 4. Determine Runner-Up

At the end of the current round, determine which non-winning Queens qualify.

A Queen qualifies for Runner-Up if:

- their recorded Appeal meets or exceeds the active Stage's Slay Target; and
- they satisfy any normal Stage prerequisite that would be required to Slay, except that they do not claim the Stage trophy or receive the normal Stage Winner reward.

Among qualifying Queens, the Queen with the **highest recorded Appeal** becomes **Runner-Up**.

If no other Queen qualifies, there is no Runner-Up and proceed directly to Curtain Call.

#### Runner-Up tie

If two or more qualifying Queens are tied for highest recorded Appeal, break the tie using active-player order: starting with the current active player and proceeding clockwise, the first tied Queen encountered becomes Runner-Up.

Record ties during testing; this tiebreak may change if it proves unsatisfying.

### 5. Runner-Up Dragdagulan† opportunity

The Runner-Up may immediately challenge **any eligible player** to Dragdagulan†.

This challenge is **optional**.

If the Runner-Up declines, proceed to Curtain Call.

If accepted by the rules, resolve Dragdagulan† using the normal current v7.8 battle rules:

1. Choose an eligible opponent who is not Untouchable.
2. Each player draws 3 battle cards from their personal Deck.
3. Battle Score = printed LS on those three cards + explicit active `+LS in battle` modifiers.
4. Equipped Masters and Deep Storage do not automatically add LS.
5. Higher Battle Score wins.
6. Resolve the normal winner reward and loser penalty.
7. Archive the battle-drawn cards.

This experimental module does **not** change the LS calculation, normal Dragdagulan† rewards, or penalties unless a separate test explicitly says so.

### 6. Curtain Call

Under this alternate module, **Curtain Call occurs only after**:

- the Stage Winner has been determined;
- the current round has finished;
- Runner-Up has been determined, if any; and
- the Runner-Up's Dragdagulan† opportunity has been resolved or declined.

Then resolve normal Curtain Call cleanup and reveal the next Stage.

## Short reference

**Stage opens → First Slay = Stage Winner → Finish round → Determine Runner-Up → Optional Runner-Up Dragdagulan† → Curtain Call → Next Stage**

## What does not change

Unless specifically stated above, keep the normal v7.8 rules for:

- Tenets and Brands;
- Shopping;
- Matching Looks;
- Perfect Illusion;
- Fusion;
- Queen abilities and Special Appeals;
- Stage prerequisites;
- LS calculation;
- Dragdagulan† rewards and penalties;
- Final Judging and Final Score.

## Important experimental rule: first place must stay valuable

The Runner-Up does **not**:

- claim a second Stage trophy;
- gain the Stage's normal Slay reward;
- count as having Slayed that Stage unless a later test explicitly changes this.

The Stage Winner remains the sole Stage trophy holder.

## Playtest data to record

For every Stage tested with this module, record:

- who became Stage Winner;
- Winner's Appeal at Slay;
- whether a Runner-Up qualified;
- Runner-Up's recorded Appeal;
- whether there was a Runner-Up tie;
- whether Runner-Up used the Dragdagulan† opportunity;
- who they challenged;
- both Battle Scores;
- battle outcome and penalties/rewards;
- extra time added by the post-Slay sequence;
- whether players understood when Curtain Call happened;
- whether the first-place Stage win still felt valuable;
- whether the Runner-Up opportunity made players stay engaged;
- whether Dragdagulan† occurred more often than under standard rules.

## Questions after the game

1. Did having two placements make the Stage feel more like an event?
2. Did the Runner-Up opportunity feel earned?
3. Did finishing the round after the first Slay feel exciting or merely delay the game?
4. Did the optional Dragdagulan† create a satisfying climax?
5. Did players still care strongly about being the first Queen to Slay?
6. Was recording Appeal until the end of the round easy enough?
7. Did the rule create a turn-order advantage?
8. Should Runner-Up be able to challenge the Stage Winner, or only another player?
9. Should Runner-Up receive any small non-SP reward even if they decline the battle?
10. Would you prefer this system to the standard immediate-Curtain-Call rule?

## A/B testing recommendation

Compare at least:

- one game using standard v7.8 Stage/Curtain Call rules; and
- one game with the same player count using this alternate module.

Do not replace the canonical Stage system based on one session. Treat this as a **watching/test** rule until repeated play data shows whether it improves interaction, pacing, and drama.
