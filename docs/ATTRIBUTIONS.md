# Haute & Hazard — Attributions and Inspiration Notes

## Legal/IP review marker

Any term or element marked with **†** is **working prototype material subject to legal/IP, trademark, and publisher review before commercial release**. A marked term may be retained, cleared, licensed if necessary, or replaced before publication.

## Dragdagulan†

The term **“Dragdagulan”† is not original to Haute & Hazard**. It is used by the Filipino drag competition series **Drag Den Philippines** for its head-to-head drag/lip-sync showdown format and has been publicly associated with the show and its promotional language.

Haute & Hazard currently uses **Dragdagulan†** as working prototype terminology for its own original card-game battle mechanic. The rules, scoring procedure, card interactions, rewards, penalties, and other gameplay implementation in Haute & Hazard are original to this project.

**Haute & Hazard is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or licensed by Drag Den Philippines, Manila Luzon, Prime Video, or any related producers, distributors, creators, or rights holders.**

This attribution is included to clearly acknowledge the source of the term and avoid presenting it as an original Haute & Hazard coinage.

### Publication / rights note

Attribution does not itself resolve every possible trademark, branding, or other intellectual-property issue. Before commercial publication, the publisher/designer should conduct an appropriate rights review of **Dragdagulan†** and either obtain any permission that may be required or replace the term with an original Haute & Hazard name if advised.

## General inspiration policy

Haute & Hazard may draw broad inspiration from drag performance culture, fashion games, deck-building games, and televised competition formats, but its rules text, characters, Brands, Tenets, card systems, scoring, and game-world material are intended to be original unless an attribution or **† legal/IP-review** note says otherwise.
