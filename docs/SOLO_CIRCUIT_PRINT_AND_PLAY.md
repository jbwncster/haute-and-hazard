# Haute & Hazard — Solo Circuit v0.2 Print & Play

**Status:** experimental one-player module for the v7.9 base game; current physical package is committed.

Solo Circuit v0.2 uses **30 separate solo cards**:

- 18 Automa cards
- 12 House Queen Personality cards

These cards are not part of the 240-card base-game Poker Deck.

## Printing

Print the Solo card PDFs:

- US Letter
- **Actual Size / 100%**
- do not use Fit to Page

For easiest assembly, print single-sided and sleeve each paper front with an opaque sleeve and spare card/cardstock behind it.

If duplex printing, test one sheet first to confirm your printer's flip-edge/alignment behavior.

- v7.9 play-generated Tip rules;
- four-round Stage Timer.
