# Haute & Hazard v7.9 — Beginner Mode

Beginner Mode is the recommended first game. It uses the normal v7.9 Queens, starter decks, five-card Wardrobe Rack, Thrift Store, Penalties, **play-generated Tips**, Shopping economy, Look rules, Slay rules, and Dragdagulan†.

The only simplification is how you read each Stage.

> **† Legal/IP review:** `Dragdagulan` is working prototype terminology credited to *Drag Den Philippines* and remains subject to legal/IP, trademark, and publisher review.

## Use these Stage fields

1. **Favored Tenet**
2. **Featured Brand**
3. **Slay Target**
4. **Reward**
5. **Venue Effect**

## Ignore these advanced Stage fields

- Judge
- Spotlight Requirement
- Judge's Favor
- Brand Ovation

No cards need to be removed or reprinted.

## Everything else stays active

Players still use:

- Tenets and Brands
- Face / Wig / Body / Shoes Coordinates
- Matching Look
- Perfect Illusion
- Fusion
- play-generated Tips
- five-card hands
- Shopping
- Appeal
- Slay
- LS and Dragdagulan†
- Penalties
- Queen Signature Abilities
- once-per-game Special Appeals
- Gross SP and the normal end trigger

## Recommended teaching setup

For the easiest first game, use **2 players**.

Each player receives:

- 7 × Basic Beat — printed Tip 1
- 3 × Messy Lip Sync — printed Tip 0
- 2 × Chapstick — printed Tip 1

Shuffle each 12-card starter deck and draw **5 cards**. Set the Wardrobe Rack to **5 face-up cards**.

Shuffle the 12 venue Stages and point out only the five Beginner Mode fields.

## v7.9 turn structure

1. **Transformation** — play cards from hand one at a time, in any order. Each played card generates its printed Tips, then resolves its text.
2. **The Reveal** — total Appeal, determine the Look, and resolve Reveal effects.
3. **Shopping** — spend Tips to buy any number of legal affordable cards. Purchased cards go to the Backstage Archive.
4. **Slay / Dragdagulan† / Pass**
5. **Cleanup** — archive played non-Masters and unplayed hand cards, refill the Wardrobe Rack to five, draw a fresh hand of 5, and reset temporary Tips/Appeal.

A card left unplayed in hand generates no printed Tips. A Master carried over from an earlier turn does not generate its printed Tips again.

## Slaying a Stage

To Slay:

1. meet or exceed the printed Slay Target;
2. satisfy any normal requirement outside the ignored advanced layer;
3. claim the Stage trophy;
4. gain the printed reward;
5. resolve Slay effects and Curtain Call.

Do not check Spotlight Requirement or award Judge's Favor/Brand Ovation in Beginner Mode.

## Moving to full Stage rules

After players understand the core loop, re-enable:

- Judge
- Spotlight Requirement
- Judge's Favor
- Brand Ovation

Nothing else changes.
