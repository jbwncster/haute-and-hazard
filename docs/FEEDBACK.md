# Haute & Hazard — Feedback Guide

Thank you for playtesting **Haute & Hazard**. Feedback is most useful when it tells us both **what happened at the table** and **how the game felt while it was happening**.

For the current physical build, use **v7.8 Game Crafter Print Edition**. If you deliberately test an older or mixed build, note that clearly.

## Quick feedback

If you do not want to write a full session report, answer these questions after the game:

1. **Which mode did you play?** Beginner Mode or Full Stage Rules?
2. **How many players were there, and how long did the game take?**
3. **Which Queens were played?**
4. **What was the most fun moment?**
5. **What was the most confusing moment?**
6. **Did Shopping feel useful and exciting?** Too cheap, too expensive, or about right?
7. **Did building a Coordinate feel satisfying?** Were Matching, Perfect Illusion, or Fusion easy to understand?
8. **Did the active Stage affect your decisions, or did it feel easy to ignore?**
9. **Did Dragdagulan feel tense and worthwhile?**
10. **Did any Queen, card, Brand, Tenet, Stage, or Special Appeal feel much stronger or weaker than the rest?**
11. **Did the game feel too short, too long, or about right?**
12. **Would you play again? Why or why not?**

## Rate the experience

A simple 1–5 rating is useful for comparing multiple playtests.

| Category | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rules clarity | Very confusing | Needed some references | Easy to follow |
| Turn flow | Frequently stalled | Mostly smooth | Very smooth |
| Shopping | Uninteresting/frustrating | Functional | Exciting decisions |
| Fashion/Coordinate building | Hard to use | Worked | Highly satisfying |
| Stage interaction | Mostly ignored | Sometimes mattered | Drove decisions |
| Queen identity | Felt generic | Some identity | Strongly distinct |
| Dragdagulan | Flat/confusing | Fine | Tense and memorable |
| Player interaction | Too little/awkward | About right | Excellent |
| Game length | Poor fit | Acceptable | Felt right |
| Overall fun | Would not replay | Might replay | Want to play again |

## Beginner Mode feedback

If you used **Beginner Mode**, pay special attention to whether the reduced Stage text made the game easier to learn.

Beginner Mode uses only:

- Favored Tenet
- Featured Brand
- Slay Target
- Reward
- Venue Effect

Ignore Judge, Spotlight Requirement, Judge's Favor, and Brand Ovation in Beginner Mode.

After the game, ask:

- Did the five active Stage fields feel manageable?
- Were Favored Tenet and Featured Brand easy to distinguish?
- Did players understand how to Slay a Stage without help?
- Were players ready to add the advanced Stage layer after one game?
- Was anything else still creating too much first-game complexity?

## Full Stage Rules feedback

When using the complete Stage system, specifically note:

- how often Spotlight Requirements were met;
- how often Judge's Favors were earned;
- how often Brand Ovations were earned;
- whether those bonuses changed deck-building or Coordinate decisions;
- whether the extra Stage information felt rewarding or merely busy;
- whether any Stage was consistently easier or harder than its Slay Target suggested.

## Shopping and deck-building feedback

The Shopping economy is one of the game's core systems. Record anything that affects whether it is working as intended:

- Were players usually able to buy something useful?
- Did players have meaningful choices among the five Wardrobe Rack cards?
- Did players save or waste Tips often?
- Were Thrift Store Throwbacks attractive enough to consider?
- Did any Cost feel obviously too high or too low?
- Did purchased cards enter players' hands quickly enough to feel rewarding?
- Did the starter deck create a clear progression into a more distinctive personal deck?

## Fashion, Tenets, and Brands feedback

Please note whether players could quickly understand the difference between a **Tenet** and a **Brand**.

Useful observations include:

- how long it took to assemble the first Complete Coordinate;
- frequency of Matching Looks;
- frequency of Perfect Illusions;
- frequency of Fusion Looks;
- whether any Tenet or Brand appeared easier to build around;
- whether Fashion slot availability ever prevented a player from participating meaningfully;
- whether Wild or Fusion interactions required repeated rulebook checks.

## Queen feedback

Every Queen should feel distinct without making one player start with an overwhelming advantage.

Record:

- whether the Signature Ability mattered during the game;
- whether the Special Appeal was used;
- when the Special Appeal was used and whether it felt exciting;
- whether the Queen encouraged a recognizable strategy;
- whether the Queen's Favorite Brand and Signature Tenet felt relevant;
- whether any ability wording caused a timing dispute;
- whether the Queen felt too weak, too strong, or appropriately powerful.

## Dragdagulan feedback

Dragdagulan should feel like a high-stakes confrontation rather than an automatic choice.

Please record:

- who challenged whom;
- Battle Scores when possible;
- whether the challenger had a meaningful reason to initiate the battle;
- whether LS on the three battle-drawn cards was easy to calculate;
- whether penalties and winner rewards felt proportionate;
- whether players avoided Dragdagulan entirely and why;
- any interaction that made it unclear which LS modifiers counted.

Remember: equipped Fashion does not automatically add its LS to Battle Score. Only printed LS on the three battle-drawn cards and explicit active battle modifiers count.

## Rules clarity and terminology

When something is confusing, quote the exact term, card, or rule if possible. Especially flag confusion involving:

- Tenet vs. Brand
- Tip vs. Appeal vs. Gross SP vs. LS
- Master / Coordinate / Complete Coordinate
- Matching / Perfect Illusion / Fusion
- Backstage Archive
- Wardrobe Rack
- Thrift Store
- Slay
- Dragdagulan
- Curtain Call
- Penalties
- Queen Signature Ability vs. Special Appeal

A useful rules report explains **what the players were trying to do**, **what wording caused uncertainty**, and **how the table resolved it**.

## Balance feedback

Please separate the observation from the proposed solution.

Good example:

> "Opulencia bought three high-Cost cards by turn four and finished 9 SP ahead. Her Shopping ability triggered four times."

Less useful by itself:

> "Nerf Opulencia."

One unusual game may be variance. Repeated patterns across several tests are especially valuable.

## What to record for a full playtest

A detailed report should include:

- version and mode;
- player count;
- game duration;
- Queens used;
- winner and final scores;
- Stage trophies;
- notable Shopping purchases;
- Look-state frequency;
- Judge's Favors and Brand Ovations, if using Full Stage Rules;
- Dragdagulan attempts and results;
- penalties gained;
- Signature Ability and Special Appeal usage;
- confusing rules or card text;
- strongest and weakest-feeling cards or strategies;
- most fun moment;
- one thing you would change;
- whether the group would play again.

## Submit feedback

Use the repository's **Playtest report** issue form:

https://github.com/jbwncster/haute-and-hazard/issues/new?template=playtest-report.yml

For general playtest guidance, see [`../CONTRIBUTING.md`](../CONTRIBUTING.md). For the current rules summary, see [`CURRENT_GAMEPLAY.md`](CURRENT_GAMEPLAY.md). For first games, see [`BEGINNER_MODE.md`](BEGINNER_MODE.md).

The goal of playtest feedback is not to prove that an idea is good or bad. It is to identify where the game is **fun, clear, tense, slow, confusing, repetitive, or unexpectedly strong**, so those patterns can guide the next revision.
