# Haute & Hazard — Solo Circuit Automa Playtest

**Status:** Experimental playtest module. This is **not canonical v7.8** and should not be used to judge normal multiplayer balance without labeling the session as a Solo Circuit test.

> **† Legal/IP review:** `Dragdagulan` is working prototype terminology credited to *Drag Den Philippines*. It is subject to legal/IP, trademark, and publisher review before commercial release and may be renamed. See [ATTRIBUTIONS.md](ATTRIBUTIONS.md).

## Purpose

Solo Circuit is a one-player development mode inspired by low-upkeep automa systems: the human player uses the normal Haute & Hazard deck-building rules while an automated rival, the **House Queen**, uses a small behavior deck instead of a full hand, deck, or Coordinate.

The design goal is simple:

> **You play Haute & Hazard. The House Queen plays a streamlined simulation of Haute & Hazard.**

This lets one person test Stage pacing, Shopping pressure, Queen performance, Dragdagulan† incentives, and scoring without controlling a second full player.

## Automa card anatomy

Each Automa card should contain four functional areas:

1. **Spotlight / Appeal** — Appeal generated by the House Queen.
2. **Wardrobe** — which card the House Queen removes from the five-card Market Row.
3. **Lip Sync** — printed LS used when resolving Dragdagulan†.
4. **Behavior / Effect** — a special instruction for that round.

Example:

> **THE CROWD ERUPTS**  
> Appeal: +4  
> Wardrobe: Remove the highest-cost Fashion from the Market Row.  
> LS: 5  
> Effect: If the removed card matches the active Stage's Featured Brand, House Queen gains +1 Appeal this round.

A card removed by the House Queen goes to a face-up **House Wardrobe** pile, then refill the Market Row normally. The House Wardrobe is primarily a record of market pressure unless a Personality or Automa effect references it.

## Solo setup

1. Set up one human player using the normal current playtest rules.
2. Choose a Queen for the player.
3. Choose a different Queen for the House Queen, or use a generic House Queen for early testing.
4. Shuffle the 18-card Automa deck.
5. Set the House Queen to **0 Gross SP** and no Penalties.
6. Set up the normal Wardrobe Rack, Market Row, Thrift Store, Penalties, and Stage deck.
7. For the easiest first solo test, use **Beginner Mode** Stage fields.
8. Select a difficulty.

## Difficulty

Use one Automa deck and modify its printed results:

| Difficulty | Modifier |
| --- | --- |
| **Local Girl** | −2 House Appeal; −1 House LS |
| **Working Queen** | Printed values |
| **Headliner** | +1 House Appeal |
| **Legend** | +2 House Appeal; +1 House LS |

**Working Queen** is the baseline for balance testing. House Appeal and House LS cannot be reduced below 0.

## Solo round sequence

At the beginning of each solo round:

### 1. Reveal an Automa card

Reveal the top House Queen card and resolve its Wardrobe instruction. Refill the Market Row immediately if a card was removed.

Keep the Automa card face-up for its Appeal, LS, and effect.

### 2. Take the player's normal turn

Use the rules for the build being tested.

For synchronized v7.8 testing:

**Transformation → Reveal → Shopping → Slay / Dragdagulan† / Pass → Cleanup**

For an explicitly labeled v7.9 Tip-economy test, use the current v7.9 candidate timing instead.

### 3. Resolve the House Queen's Stage performance

The House Queen's Stage Appeal is:

**House Base Appeal + revealed Automa Appeal + applicable effects + difficulty modifier**

For the first prototype, give each Stage a temporary **House Base Appeal** on the Solo reference sheet rather than altering the physical Stage cards. These values require balance testing and are not yet canonical.

### 4. Check the Stage

If the player Slays the Stage during their normal Phase 4, resolve the player's Slay and Curtain Call normally.

If the player does not Slay and the House Queen meets or exceeds the Stage's Slay Target, the House Queen claims that Stage as a trophy, gains its printed Gross SP reward, and triggers Curtain Call.

If neither Queen Slays it, leave the Stage active and begin another solo round.

### Stage timer

For the first A/B test, place a round marker on the Stage each time neither side Slays it.

After **three unsuccessful rounds**, the House Queen automatically claims the Stage and triggers Curtain Call.

Track whether this rule feels fair; it is deliberately provisional.

## Solo Dragdagulan†

The human player uses the normal battle procedure. The House Queen substitutes Automa cards for a personal deck.

1. The player challenges the House Queen during Phase 4.
2. The player draws **3 battle cards** from their personal Deck normally.
3. Reveal **3 Automa cards** for the House Queen.
4. Player Battle Score = normal printed LS and eligible modifiers.
5. House Battle Score = total printed LS on the three revealed Automa cards + difficulty modifier + explicit House effects.
6. Higher score wins.
7. Archive/discard the player's battle cards normally and place the three battle Automa cards in the Automa discard pile.

### If the player wins

Use the normal winner choice where possible:

- steal **1 Gross SP** from the House Queen; or
- give the House Queen a **Wardrobe Malfunction**.

### If the House Queen wins

The active/revealed Automa effect should specify its preferred consequence. During early testing, if no consequence is printed, use:

- House Queen steals **1 Gross SP** if the player has at least 1; otherwise
- player takes **Wardrobe Malfunction** if available.

### Tie

Use the normal Dragdagulan† tie consequence as closely as possible: both sides receive **The Chop** if available and neither receives a winner reward.

## House Queen behavior families

The 18-card deck should mix recognizable behavior types rather than behave randomly without personality:

- **Fashion Hunter** — attacks desirable Market cards.
- **Spotlight Hog** — produces stronger Appeal.
- **Tip Snatcher** — disrupts or constrains Shopping.
- **Lip Sync Assassin** — carries stronger LS.
- **Drama Queen** — creates disruptive one-round effects.

The discard pile remains visible so the player can gradually infer what behaviors remain in the Automa deck.

## Queen Personality cards

After the generic Automa works, test a small House Personality rule for each of the 12 Queens. These should evoke the Queen without making the solo player operate a full second tableau.

Examples:

### Lady Lux — Treasure From Trash
Whenever the House Queen removes a Market card costing 2 or less, gain **+1 House Appeal** this round.

### Gore-Jess — Final Girl
Whenever the House Queen wins Dragdagulan†, gain **+1 Gross SP**.

### Kawaii-Bot — Idol Perfect
Whenever the House Queen removes a Pink Fashion card, place 1 Match token on Kawaii-Bot. At 3 Match tokens, gain **+1 Gross SP**, then discard those tokens.

These are **prototype examples**, not final Personality balance.

## Final Judging

Use the normal end trigger for the build being tested.

The human player calculates Final Score normally.

For the first Automa prototype, the House Queen calculates:

**Gross SP + explicit House end-game bonuses − Penalty values**

Stage trophies are already represented by their printed Gross SP rewards and should not be scored a second time unless a future Personality explicitly awards a trophy bonus.

Highest Final Score wins. Apply the normal tiebreak sequence where it can be resolved for both sides; record any case where the Automa cannot cleanly use a tiebreak.

## What this mode should test

Solo Circuit should help answer:

- Does the Market feel contested with only one human player?
- Does the House Queen Slay too often or too rarely?
- Are three rounds per Stage too short or too long?
- Does Dragdagulan† feel worth attempting against the Automa?
- Are House LS values predictable enough to support decisions without becoming trivial?
- Does a House Personality make the opponent feel like a Queen without adding bookkeeping?
- Which player Queens consistently over- or under-perform?
- Which Stages create unusually large win-rate swings?
- Is Working Queen an appropriate baseline difficulty?

## Development gates

Do not call Solo Circuit an official 1-player mode until:

1. the 18-card distribution has been tested across all four difficulties;
2. multiple player Queens have been tested against generic and Personality House Queens;
3. House Base Appeal values exist for all 12 Stages;
4. Dragdagulan† results have been logged across enough battles to judge LS distribution;
5. the mode has been tested in both physical Print & Play and TTS form;
6. the solo rules can be taught without requiring knowledge of hidden designer assumptions.

Until those gates are met, label all materials:

> **SOLO CIRCUIT — EXPERIMENTAL PLAYTEST MODULE**
