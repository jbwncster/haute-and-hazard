## Special Appeals — once per game

Use a marker to show when a Queen's Special Appeal has been spent.

### Lady Lux — Trash to Treasure
Once per game during Transformation, return up to 2 eligible cards from your Personal Trash to your hand. Gain 1 Tip for each returned Yellow card.

### Kawaii-Bot — Idol Overdrive
Once per game during The Reveal, if you have a Matching Pink Look, improve the Reveal result for this turn: a normal Matching ×2 becomes ×3. If you already have Perfect Illusion, gain +3 Appeal instead.

### Mother Mortis — Raise the Dead
Once per game during Transformation, equip one eligible Fashion card from your Backstage Archive as though it were in your hand. Resolve its Play ability normally and ignore any additional effect cost specifically required to equip that card.

### Opulencia — No Budget, No Problem
Once per game during Shopping, reduce the cost of one Wardrobe purchase by 4 Tips, minimum 0. If the purchased card is Gilded Cage, draw 1 card afterward.

### Velvet Venom — Death Drop
Once per game when an opponent targets you with a hostile effect, cancel that effect. That opponent then loses 1 Tip, minimum 0.

### Maldita Manila — Dual Crown
Once per game during The Reveal, choose two non-Neutral Tenets represented in your Coordinate. For this Reveal, effects that check your Look's Tenet may treat it as either chosen Tenet. This does not satisfy a named Brand requirement by itself.

### Hexa Laroux — Perfect Geometry
Once per game during The Reveal, if all four core slots are occupied, choose one Master and one other Master. Add the first Master's printed Appeal to the second Master's contribution for this Reveal only.

### Bubblegum Banshee — Sugar Rush Encore
Once per game during Shopping, draw 3 cards, keep 1 in hand, and archive the other 2. If the kept card is Pink, gain 1 Tip before Shopping ends.

### Siren Diesel — Full Throttle
Once per game when you enter Dragdagulan†, each of your three battle-drawn cards gains +1 LS for that battle.

### Opal Dynasty — Full Spectrum
Once per game during The Reveal, choose one Master and treat it as Wild for this Reveal. Assign it one active non-Neutral Tenet under the normal Wild rules.

### Rapture Royale — Divine Sacrifice
Once per game during Transformation, trash up to 2 eligible cards from your hand. Gain 2 Tips and 1 Appeal for each card trashed this way.

### Gore-Jess — Final Girl Frenzy
Once per game after you win Dragdagulan†, you may take 2 Gross SP instead of the normal winner reward. If you won by 5 or more Battle Score, take 3 Gross SP instead. Her normal Final Girl Signature Ability still resolves if its condition was met.

## Character direction

The Queens are full drag personas first, with camp, horror, fashion, nightlife, alternative-drag, and queer-culture archetypes informing their presentation. The game's visual and tonal inspiration can draw from drag competition spectacle, underground club culture, horror glamour, fashion editorial, and arcade-idol coordination while retaining Haute & Hazard's own names, mechanics, world, Tenets, Brands, and terminology except where a **† legal/IP-review marker** explicitly indicates a working or attributed element.
