# Haute & Hazard — Publisher Pitch Deck

Use this as the slide script for an 8–10 slide publisher presentation.

## Slide 1 — Cover

**Haute & Hazard**  
*Build the look. Work the runway. Destroy the competition.*

2–5 players · competitive deck-building · drag fashion strategy · active v7.8 prototype

## Slide 2 — Elevator pitch

Haute & Hazard is a competitive drag fashion deck-building game where players become distinct Queens, build wardrobes from a shared market, assemble coordinated Looks, Slay venue Stages, and challenge rivals in Dragdagulan† Lip Sync battles.

## Slide 3 — The hook

**Fashion is the strategy.**

Players are not only buying cards. They are constructing a persistent on-table Coordinate that changes how they score, shop, battle, and respond to the active venue.

## Slide 4 — Core loop

Transform → Reveal → Shop → Slay / Dragdagulan† / Pass → Cleanup

Show how one turn moves from building a Look to gaining resources to taking a tactical action.

## Slide 5 — Coordinates, Tenets, and Brands

- Four core Fashion slots: Face, Wig, Body, Shoes.
- Accessory is optional.
- Tenets are color/faction identities.
- Brands are named fashion lines inside Tenets.
- Matching Looks, Perfect Illusions, and Fusion Looks create different strategic paths.

## Slide 6 — Queens

12 asymmetric Queens with:

- Signature Tenet
- Favorite Brand
- Signature Ability
- Special Appeal — once per game

Queen identity should encourage strategy without overwhelming first-time players.

## Slide 7 — Stages and interaction

12 venue-style Stages reward different Tenets/Brands and create changing objectives.

Players can either Slay the current Stage or confront a rival through Dragdagulan†.

Beginner Mode removes the advanced Judge/Spotlight layer for first games.

## Slide 8 — Components and build status

Current v7.8 prototype includes:

- 240-card Poker Deck
- 12 venue Stages
- 12 Jumbo Queens
- 5 player aids
- physical Game Crafter prototype
- aligned Tabletop Simulator build
- structured feedback templates

## Slide 9 — Playtest plan

Current focus:

- first-time learnability
- game length
- Queen balance
- Shopping satisfaction
- Stage pressure
- Dragdagulan† frequency and payoff
- replay interest

Target evidence: 10+ external v7.8 plays followed by a State of the Prototype report.

## Slide 10 — What I am looking for

A publisher interested in a visually distinctive, interactive hobby game and willing to evaluate a prototype still open to development.

Publisher development, editing, art direction, production adjustments, terminology review, and balance work are welcome.
