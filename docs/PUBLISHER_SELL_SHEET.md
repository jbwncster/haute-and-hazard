# HAUTE & HAZARD
### *Build the look. Work the runway. Destroy the competition.*

**2–5 players · Competitive deck-building · Fashion coordination · Direct player interaction · Active prototype playtesting**

> **† Legal/IP review:** `Dragdagulan` is working prototype terminology credited to *Drag Den Philippines*. It is subject to legal/IP, trademark, and publisher review before commercial release and may be renamed. See [`ATTRIBUTIONS.md`](ATTRIBUTIONS.md).

## The pitch

**Haute & Hazard** is a competitive drag fashion deck-building game in which players become larger-than-life Queens building wardrobes, assembling coordinated Looks, earning Tips, Slaying venue Stages, and challenging rivals in dramatic **Dragdagulan†** Lip Sync battles.

Players begin with identical 12-card Dressing Room Floor decks and develop distinct strategies through a shared Wardrobe market. Fashion cards fill **Face, Wig, Body, Shoes, and Accessory** slots and belong to different **Tenets** and **Brands**, rewarding players for building Matching Looks, Perfect Illusions, and two-Tenet Fusion combinations.

The result is a game about **fashion as strategy**: what you wear affects how you score, how you shop, how you perform, and how you confront the competition.

## Why it stands out

- **Drag performance is the game system, not just the theme.** Fashion coordination, runway performance, shopping, rivalries, and Lip Sync battles are mechanically connected.
- **Twelve asymmetric Queens** give players distinctive identities, Signature Abilities, and once-per-game Special Appeals.
- **Deck-building feeds a persistent Coordinate.** Players are not only improving a deck; they are constructing an evolving on-table Look.
- **Tenets and Brands create multiple build paths.** Players can chase Matching and Perfect Illusion strategies, create Fusion Looks, or pivot around the active venue.
- **Direct interaction has stakes.** **Dragdagulan†** battles use LS values drawn from each player's personal deck and can swing Gross Style Points or add Penalties.
- **Beginner Mode lowers the first-game rules load** without removing the core deck-building, fashion, Queen, or battle systems.

## Core turn

**TIP COUNT → TRANSFORM → REVEAL → SHOP → SLAY / DRAGDAGULAN† / PASS → CLEANUP**

Count printed Tips from the cards in hand, build your Coordinate, reveal its Appeal, spend Tips to strengthen your wardrobe, then either take the Stage or take on a rival. Printed Tips are counted once; playing or equipping the same card does not pay them again.

## Current prototype

The current **v7.9 playtest target** includes:

- 12 playable Queens
- 5 identical 12-card starter decks
- a shared 144-card Wardrobe Rack
- 16 Thrift Store Throwbacks
- 20 Penalty cards
- 12 venue-style Stage cards
- player aids, Beginner Mode, and structured playtest feedback tools

The **v7.9 physical and Tabletop Simulator production targets** use the same card pool, venue Stages, Queen roster, Special Appeals, Player Aid wording, Tip Count economy, and core rules. Current rules documentation, production information, TTS source/status files, playtest materials, and legacy builds are maintained through the project repository.

v7.8 and the older v7.7/v7.7.1 packages remain archived for comparison only; current multiplayer balance testing should use synchronized v7.9 materials.

An **experimental one-player Solo Circuit v0.2** is also in active development. It uses an 18-card House Queen Automa, 12 optional Queen Personality cards, four difficulty levels, a four-round Stage timer, and explicit House Appeal/scoring rules. Solo remains a development experiment and does **not** change the base game's 2–5 player claim.

## Design provenance

The project began while Jake was **watching the *Aikatsu!* anime**, which led him to discover **Data Carddass Aikatsu!** and its fashion-card/coordination structure. Later, getting into the **DC Deck-Building Game** suggested the deck-building progression that could turn that fashion idea into a tabletop game. Haute & Hazard transforms those influences into an original drag-centered design with its own slots, Tenets, Brands, Shopping economy, persistent Coordinate, Stages, Queens, scoring, and rivalry systems. See [`DESIGN_ORIGIN_AND_INSPIRATION.md`](DESIGN_ORIGIN_AND_INSPIRATION.md) for the full design-history explanation and [`ATTRIBUTIONS.md`](ATTRIBUTIONS.md) for attribution/IP notes.

## Development status

**Haute & Hazard is a playable prototype in active iterative playtesting, not a finished commercial product.** Balance, pacing, wording, component counts, graphic design, advanced Stage systems, and marked **† legal/IP-review elements** remain open to development.

The core identity is established: **drag + fashion coordination + deck-building + performance + rivalry**. The goal is to find the strongest commercial version of that experience with the right publishing partner.

The Queens, Brands, Tenets, card systems, scoring, and game-world material are intended as original **Haute & Hazard** material. Any exception or working element requiring rights review is explicitly marked **†** and documented in [`ATTRIBUTIONS.md`](ATTRIBUTIONS.md). Prototype art and presentation are subject to change during publisher development.

## Looking for

A publisher interested in a visually distinctive, interactive hobby game and willing to evaluate a prototype while it is still being refined. Publisher development, editing, art direction, production changes, legal/IP review, and further balance work are welcome.
