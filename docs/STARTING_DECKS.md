# Haute & Hazard — Starting Decks

**Applies to the current v7.9 base game.**

Every player begins with the same **12-card Dressing Room Floor starter deck**. The physical game contains **60 starter cards total**, enough for five identical starter decks.

## Starter deck composition

| Qty | Card | Type | Printed Tips | Basic role |
|---:|---|---|---:|---|
| 7 | **Basic Beat** | Action | 1 | Reliable early Shopping power through Tip Count. |
| 3 | **Messy Lip Sync** | Action | 0 | Basic performance power; its play effect provides Appeal rather than a printed Tip. |
| 2 | **Chapstick** | Makeup / Face Fashion | 1 | Early Face Fashion plus hand filtering. |

**Total: 12 cards per player.**

Across five starter decks:

- 35 Basic Beat
- 15 Messy Lip Sync
- 10 Chapstick
- **60 starter cards total**

## Critical v7.9 Tip rule

At the start of each turn, before Transformation, perform **Tip Count** and add the printed Tip values of every card currently in your hand.

The same card may then be played or equipped normally.

**Do not gain its printed Tip value again when you play/equip it.**

This means legacy wording such as “Basic Beat: gain 1 Tip when played” or “Chapstick: gain 1 Tip when equipped” must not be used in a v7.9 test if that Tip duplicates the printed value.

## Setup

1. Make identical 12-card starter decks: **7 Basic Beat, 3 Messy Lip Sync, 2 Chapstick**.
2. Give one to each player.
3. Return unused starter decks to the box; do not shuffle them into the Wardrobe Rack.
4. Each player shuffles their personal Deck.
5. Each player draws **5 cards**.
6. Each player begins with **0 Gross SP, 0 Tips, and 0 Appeal**.
7. Continue normal setup with Queens, Stage, Wardrobe Rack, Thrift Store, Penalties, and First Player marker.

## Why the decks are identical

A player's first strategic difference comes from their **Queen**, then from Shopping and deck-building rather than unequal starting cards.

There are no Queen-specific starter decks in v7.9.

## What the starter cards teach

### Basic Beat — Tip Count and Shopping

Basic Beat's **printed Tip 1** makes the pre-Transformation Tip Count reliable. Its printed Tip is not a separate play trigger.

### Messy Lip Sync — performance

Messy Lip Sync has **printed Tip 0** and teaches that a turn is not only about Shopping. Its performance effect contributes Appeal when used as instructed by the card.

### Chapstick — Fashion and hand filtering

Chapstick has **printed Tip 1** and is Face Fashion. It can occupy the Face slot and smooth an awkward hand through its filtering effect.

Its printed Tip was already counted during Tip Count, so equipping it does not generate that same Tip again.

## Deck growth

Purchased Wardrobe and Thrift cards go to the player's **Backstage Archive**. When the personal Deck runs out, shuffle the Archive to make a new Deck.

The v7.9 growth loop is:

**draw hand → Tip Count → play/equip → Shop → purchases enter Archive → reshuffle later → draw upgraded cards**

## Coordinate growth

A Complete Coordinate requires:

- Face
- Wig
- Body
- Shoes

The starter deck contains Face Fashion only through Chapstick. Players build the rest of the Coordinate through Shopping.

## Dragdagulan†

Dragdagulan† uses printed **LS** on the three battle-drawn cards plus explicit battle modifiers. Printed Tips, Appeal, and equipped-card stats do not automatically add to Battle Score.

## Sorting

Keep these groups separate:

- Dressing Room Floor starter cards
- Wardrobe Rack
- Thrift Store Throwbacks
- Penalty supply
- Stage deck
- Queen deck
- optional Solo Circuit deck

The 60 starter cards are part of the base **240-card Poker Deck** component even though they are divided into personal decks during setup.
