# Haute & Hazard — Starting Decks

**Applies to the current v7.8 Game Crafter Print Edition.**

Every player begins Haute & Hazard with the same **12-card Dressing Room Floor starter deck**. The starter deck gives each Queen a small amount of Shopping power, Appeal, and card filtering before the player begins building a stronger personal deck from the Wardrobe Rack.

The physical game contains **60 starter cards total**, enough for up to 5 players: five identical copies of the 12-card starter deck.

## Starter deck composition

Each player receives exactly:

| Qty | Card | Type | Cost | Basic role |
|---:|---|---|---:|---|
| 7 | **Basic Beat** | Action | 0 | Provides **+1 Tip** and drives the early Shopping economy. |
| 3 | **Messy Lip Sync** | Action | 0 | Provides **+1 Appeal** and gives the starter deck basic performance power. |
| 2 | **Chapstick** | Makeup / Face Fashion | 0 | Provides **+1 Tip** and lets the player discard a card to draw a card, helping smooth awkward hands. |

**Total: 12 cards per player.**

Across all five starter decks, that means:

- 35 Basic Beat
- 15 Messy Lip Sync
- 10 Chapstick
- **60 starter cards total**

## Why every player starts with the same deck

The starter decks are intentionally identical. A player's first strategic difference comes from their **Queen**, not from receiving a stronger or weaker opening card pool.

Queens then begin to diverge through:

- Signature Tenet and Favorite Brand;
- Signature Ability;
- once-per-game Special Appeal;
- Wardrobe purchases;
- Thrift Store purchases;
- card interactions, Look states, and Stage choices.

There are **no Queen-specific starting decks in v7.8**.

## Setup

At the beginning of the game:

1. Separate the 60 Dressing Room Floor cards into identical 12-card starter decks: **7 Basic Beat, 3 Messy Lip Sync, and 2 Chapstick**.
2. Give one starter deck to each player.
3. Return unused starter decks to the box. **Do not shuffle unused starter cards into the Wardrobe Rack.**
4. Each player shuffles their 12-card personal Deck.
5. Each player draws **5 cards**.
6. Each player begins with **0 Gross SP, 0 Tips, and 0 Appeal**.
7. Continue normal setup with the Queen, Active Stage, Wardrobe Rack, Thrift Store, Penalty supply, and First Player marker.

## What the starter cards teach

### Basic Beat — learn to Shop

Basic Beat is the most common starter card because it creates the game's basic Shopping rhythm. Play it to generate Tips, then spend those Tips during the Shopping phase on cards from the Wardrobe Rack or other legal sources.

Seven copies make Tip generation reliable without making every opening hand identical.

### Messy Lip Sync — learn to perform

Messy Lip Sync supplies basic Appeal. It teaches that a turn is not only about buying cards: players also need enough Appeal to threaten the active Stage.

Because the starter deck contains only three copies, the player must decide how much of an early hand to devote to Shopping versus performance.

### Chapstick — learn Fashion and hand filtering

Chapstick is a **Makeup card**, and Makeup is treated as a **Face** subtype. It therefore occupies the Face slot when equipped.

Chapstick also provides a small amount of Shopping value and can trade an unwanted card in hand for a new draw. This makes it the starter deck's primary hand-smoothing tool.

Unlike Basic Beat and Messy Lip Sync, Chapstick is Fashion rather than an Action. Once equipped as the Face Master, it can remain in the Coordinate until it is replaced, removed by an effect, or cleared by Curtain Call.

## How the starter deck grows

The 12 cards are only the beginning of a player's personal deck.

During Shopping, purchased Wardrobe and Thrift cards normally go to the player's **Backstage Archive**. When the personal Deck later runs out, the Archive is shuffled to create a new Deck. This is how purchased cards enter the player's future hands.

The normal growth loop is therefore:

**play starter cards → generate Tips/Appeal → Shop → purchased cards enter Archive → reshuffle → stronger cards enter future hands**

As the game continues, the simple Dressing Room Floor deck develops into a Queen-specific collection of Fashion, Actions, Brands, Tenets, LS values, and synergies.

## Starter cards and the Coordinate

A Complete Coordinate requires four core Masters:

- Face
- Wig
- Body
- Shoes

The starter deck contains **Face Fashion only** through Chapstick. Players must acquire Wig, Body, and Shoes cards through deck-building before they can reliably assemble Complete, Matching, Perfect Illusion, or Fusion Looks.

This is intentional: the opening deck represents a Queen arriving with only the basics and building the full look during play.

## Starter cards and Dragdagulan

Dragdagulan uses the printed **LS** values on the three battle-drawn cards plus explicit active LS modifiers.

If a starter card has no printed LS value, it contributes **LS 0** when drawn for Dragdagulan. Do not add Tips, Appeal, or an equipped card's normal stats to Battle Score unless an effect explicitly says to do so.

## Important sorting rules

Keep these groups separate during setup:

- Dressing Room Floor starter cards
- Wardrobe Rack
- Thrift Store Throwbacks
- Penalty supply
- Stage deck
- Queen deck

The 60 starter cards are physically part of the Game Crafter **Haute & Hazard — 240-card Poker Deck** component, but during actual play they are divided into personal starter decks before the Wardrobe Rack is shuffled.

## Quick check before play

For every player at the table, confirm:

**7 Basic Beat + 3 Messy Lip Sync + 2 Chapstick = 12 cards**

If every player has that exact mix, the Dressing Room Floor setup is correct.
