## Short description

**Haute & Hazard** is a 2–5 player competitive drag fashion deck-building game. Players build wardrobes from a shared Market, assemble persistent Coordinates, generate Tips, Slay venue Stages, and challenge rivals in Dragdagulan† Lip Sync battles.

The current v7.9 target adds **Tip Count before Transformation** and a fully unique 144-card Wardrobe.

## v7.9 talking points

- 240-card base Poker Deck.
- 144-card Wardrobe = 130 unique Fashion + 14 unique Actions.
- Every Poker card has a printed Tip value, including 0.
- Every turn begins with **Tip Count**.
- Printed Tips are counted once from cards in hand.
- Purchased cards go to Backstage Archive and enter a future draw cycle.
- Fashion fills Face, Wig, Body, Shoes, and optional Accessory slots.
- Matching, Perfect Illusion, and Fusion reward different fashion-building approaches.
- 12 asymmetric Queens have Signature Abilities and once-per-game Special Appeals.
- 12 venue Stages change what players want to build.
- Dragdagulan† is the direct head-to-head Lip Sync system.

## Solo Circuit v0.2

Solo Circuit is an experimental one-player mode layered over v7.9.

It uses:

- 18 Automa cards;
- 12 House Queen Personality cards;
- four difficulty levels;
- a four-round Stage timer;
- House Base Appeal = `max(0, Slay Target - 6)`;
- explicit House Stage scoring.

Solo does not change the base-game 2–5 player claim until the mode clears its development gates.

## Feedback prompts

Ask:

1. Was Tip Count easy to remember?
2. Did anyone count printed Tips twice?
3. Did the unique Wardrobe make Shopping more interesting?
4. Did building a Coordinate feel satisfying?
5. Did the Stage affect what players bought or equipped?
6. Did each Queen feel distinct?
7. Did Dragdagulan† feel worthwhile?
8. Was the game length appropriate?
9. What was the most confusing moment?
10. Would you play again?

For Solo v0.2 also ask whether the House Queen felt competitive without feeling like a second player tableau.
