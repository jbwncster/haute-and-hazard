# Haute & Hazard — Streamer / Playtester Media Kit

Use this when someone streams, records, or publicly discusses a prototype session.

## Suggested stream title

**Haute & Hazard v7.8 — Prototype Playtest**

## Short description

Haute & Hazard is a competitive drag fashion deck-building game for 2–5 players. Players become asymmetric Queens, build wardrobes from a shared market, assemble coordinated Looks, Slay venue Stages, and challenge rivals in Dragdagulan† Lip Sync battles.

## One-sentence hook

**Build your deck. Assemble your look. Slay the Stage.**

## Current status

Unpublished prototype / active playtesting. Components, balance, wording, art, terminology, and legal/IP-reviewed material may change before commercial release.

## What to capture

Useful screenshots/video moments:

- full setup;
- a completed Coordinate;

## What feedback is most useful

Ask players:

1. What was the most fun moment?
2. What was the most confusing moment?
3. Did Shopping feel useful?
4. Did building a Coordinate feel satisfying?
5. Did Dragdagulan† feel tense and worthwhile?
6. Would you play again?
