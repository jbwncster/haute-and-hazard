# Haute & Hazard — Solo Circuit Automa Deck v0.2

**18-card experimental House Queen deck.** Use with [SOLO_CIRCUIT_AUTOMA_PLAYTEST.md](SOLO_CIRCUIT_AUTOMA_PLAYTEST.md).

Numbers are playtest values. When an instruction cannot be completed, skip that part. When Market cards tie for removal, choose the tied card **furthest left**.

## Reshuffle

When the Automa draw pile empties, shuffle its discard pile to form a new draw pile.

During Dragdagulan†, reveal three cards from the same Automa deck. Their **LS values count**, but their Wardrobe and normal round effects do not trigger. Discard them after battle.

If fewer than three remain, reveal what remains, reshuffle, then reveal enough to reach three.

## v0.2 balance targets

For the first 10–20 Working Queen games, record:

- House Stage win rate;
- average rounds per Stage;
- Round 4 timeout frequency;
- average House Appeal before/after modifiers;
- player purchase count per turn;
- Market cards removed by family;
- Dragdagulan† win rate;
- how often Tip Snatcher changes a purchase;
- how often House Always Wins grants Gross SP.

Do not rebalance from one game. Flag repeated patterns across several Queens and Stages.
