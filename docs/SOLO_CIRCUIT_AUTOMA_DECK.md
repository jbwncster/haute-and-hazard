# Haute & Hazard — Solo Circuit Automa Deck v0.1

**18-card experimental House Queen deck.** Use with [SOLO_CIRCUIT_AUTOMA_PLAYTEST.md](SOLO_CIRCUIT_AUTOMA_PLAYTEST.md).

These are intentionally simple enough to run without a second player tableau. Numbers are **playtest values**, not final balance.

## Deck distribution

- 4 Fashion Hunter
- 4 Spotlight Hog
- 3 Tip Snatcher
- 4 Lip Sync Assassin
- 3 Drama Queen

Average printed Appeal: **3.0**. Average printed LS: **2.5**.

When an instruction cannot be completed, skip that part of the instruction. When multiple Market cards tie for a removal instruction, remove the tied card **furthest left**.

## Automa reshuffle

When the Automa draw pile empties, shuffle its discard pile to form a new draw pile.

During a Dragdagulan†, the three battle cards are revealed from the same Automa deck. Their **LS values count**, but their Wardrobe and round effects do **not** trigger. Discard them after battle.

If fewer than three cards remain, reveal the remaining cards, reshuffle the discard pile, then reveal enough to reach three.

## First balance targets

For the first 10–20 Working Queen games, record:

- House Stage win rate;
- average rounds per Stage;
- average House Appeal before House Base Appeal;
- player purchase count per turn;
- Market cards removed by family;
- Dragdagulan† win rate;
- how often Tip Snatcher prevents a purchase rather than merely changing it;
- how often House Always Wins grants Gross SP.

Do not rebalance from one game. Flag repeated patterns across several Queens and Stages.
