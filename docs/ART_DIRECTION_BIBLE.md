# Haute & Hazard — Art Direction Bible

This is a prototype art-direction guide, not final production art.

## Visual promise

Haute & Hazard should feel like:

- drag performance as combat;
- fashion editorial colliding with nightlife spectacle;
- camp, glamour, danger, and wit;
- readable cards first, maximal style second.

## Fashion-card visual inspiration

The current card refresh may learn from the **high-level readability principles** of Data Carddass Aikatsu!: fashion presented prominently, obvious category/type identity, visible Brand identity, status/rarity cues, and a clear performance value.

That inspiration must be transformed into an original Haute & Hazard system. Do not copy Aikatsu! characters, artwork, logos, Brand marks, icons, QR treatments, card frames, exact layout geometry, typefaces, decorative motifs, or trade dress.

See [CARD_VISUAL_REFRESH.md](CARD_VISUAL_REFRESH.md) for the complete migration specification.

## Core palette

Suggested foundation:

- Black / charcoal for drama and table contrast.
- Ivory or warm off-white for readable text panels.
- Antique gold for frame, score, and prestige accents.
- Four Tenet colors kept distinct and colorblind-aware.

## Tenet direction

| Tenet | Direction |
|---|---|
| Pink | Pop, sugar, idol, digital brightness. |
| Blue | Horror, necro-glam, slasher, void, moonlit edge. |
| Purple | Luxury, velvet, royalty, trap, decadence. |
| Yellow | Trash couture, carnival, swamp, thrift alchemy. |
| Neutral | Utility, backstage, glue cards, visual restraint. |

Each Tenet must have an original symbol and written label in addition to color.

## Card readability hierarchy

1. Card name.
2. Hero fashion art.
3. Type / slot.
4. Tenet and Brand.
5. Cost, Tips, Appeal, LS, SP as applicable.
6. Rules text.
7. Card ID / prototype status / legal marker.
8. Flavor and decorative detail.

The visual redesign must preserve **all current card information**. If information does not fit, change the layout rather than deleting or abbreviating gameplay content.

Rules text must remain readable in print and TTS.

## Fashion card composition

Fashion cards should prioritize the garment, wig, makeup concept, shoe, or accessory as the hero object.

A secondary model vignette may show the item in context, but the card must use original drag/editorial art and should not imitate Aikatsu!'s character compositions.

Brand, Tenet, slot, and resource information should have fixed locations across the deck so players can scan the Wardrobe Rack quickly.

## Queen portraits

Queens should read as full drag personas, not generic fantasy classes. Each portrait should imply:

- performance identity;
- fashion silhouette;
- Signature Tenet;
- emotional attitude;
- stage presence.

Avoid leaning on real-person likenesses or recognizable celebrity references unless cleared.

## Stage cards

Stages are venues, not abstract challenges. They should quickly communicate where the performance is happening.

Examples:

- ballroom
- nightclub
- haunted hotel
- thrift superstore
- opera house
- leather bar
- arcade

Stage layouts should remain visually distinct from Fashion cards and preserve every current v7.9 Stage field.

## Iconography

Icons should support rules clarity. Do not create symbols so ornate that players have to relearn the rules from the art.

All icons must be original to Haute & Hazard. Do not trace, redraw, recolor, or closely imitate Aikatsu!, DC Deck-Building Game, or other third-party iconography.

## Accessibility rules

- Do not rely on color alone for Tenet recognition.
- Use icon and written-label support for each Tenet.
- Maintain strong text contrast.
- Keep body text large enough for tabletop distance.
- Avoid dense decorative patterns behind rules text.
- Keep Cost, Tips, Appeal, LS, and SP visually distinct in both shape and label.

## Legal/IP review

Any visual element derived from a specific third-party property, celebrity, music video, brand, show, meme, or real venue should be marked **†** and added to [IP_REVIEW_REGISTER.md](IP_REVIEW_REGISTER.md).

For the Aikatsu!-inspired visual refresh specifically, only **general design principles** may carry forward. No official Aikatsu! artwork, characters, logos, Brand marks, badges, QR-code treatments, specific decorative compositions, exact card-frame geometry, or trade dress should appear in production assets.
