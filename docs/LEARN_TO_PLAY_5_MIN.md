# Haute & Hazard v7.9 — Learn to Play in 5 Minutes

**Goal:** Build the sharpest Look, Slay venue Stages, survive rival battles, and finish with the most Style Points.

## Set up

1. Shuffle the 12 Stages and reveal 1 Active Stage.
2. Shuffle the Wardrobe deck and reveal 5 cards as the Wardrobe Rack.
3. Set out the Thrift Store and Penalties.
4. Choose Queens.
5. Each player takes a starter deck of 7 Basic Beat, 3 Messy Lip Sync, and 2 Chapstick; shuffle and draw 5.
6. Everyone starts at 0 Gross SP, 0 Tips, and 0 Appeal. Randomly choose the First Player.

Play proceeds clockwise until someone Slays the Active Stage.

## Your Coordinate

Fashion fills four core slots:

1. Face
2. Wig
3. Body
4. Shoes

Accessory is optional unless a card says otherwise. Filling all four core slots creates a **Complete Coordinate**.

## Your turn

Every turn follows this order:

1. **Transform** — play cards from hand **one at a time, in any order**. Each played card generates its printed Tips, then resolves its text. Equip Fashion, play Actions, and use eligible Queen abilities.
2. **Reveal** — total Appeal, resolve Reveal effects, and determine your Look.
3. **Shop** — spend the Tips you generated. You may buy multiple legal cards as long as you can afford them.
4. **Slay / Dragdagulan† / Pass** — take the Stage, battle a rival, or pass.
5. **Cleanup** — archive your Accessory, played non-Master cards, and every card still in hand; refill empty Wardrobe Rack spaces; draw a fresh hand of **5** if possible; and reset temporary Tips/Appeal. Core-slot Masters and legal Deep Storage remain until replaced, removed, or Curtain Call.

### Tips work like Power

A card generates its printed Tips **when you play it**, not merely because it is in your hand.

- Play a card → add its printed Tips → resolve its effect.
- Unplayed cards generate no Tips.
- Already-equipped Masters do not generate Tips again on later turns.
- A card drawn during Transformation can be played later that Transformation and generate its Tips.
- Fashion may be played for Tips without being equipped; if so, it is archived during Cleanup and does not count toward the Coordinate.

## The numbers

- **Tips** buy cards.
- **Appeal** Slays the active Stage.
- **Gross SP** is your score before final penalties.
- **LS** is used in Dragdagulan†.

## Looks

- **Matching Look:** all four core Masters share one non-Neutral Tenet.
- **Perfect Illusion:** a Matching Look that matches your Queen's Signature Tenet.
- **Fusion Look:** exactly two non-Neutral Tenets in the Coordinate/Fusion pool.

## Shopping

The Wardrobe Rack has five face-up cards. Spend Tips to buy affordable cards.

Purchased cards go to your **Backstage Archive / personal discard pile**. They do not enter your current hand; you draw them after your deck cycles.

The Wardrobe Rack starts with five face-up cards. If you buy one or more, leave those spaces empty until Cleanup, then refill back to five.

## Slay or battle

After Shopping, choose one normal Phase-4 option:

- **Slay** if your Appeal meets the Stage target.
- **Dragdagulan†** an eligible rival.
- **Pass**.

## Cleanup

Archive your Accessory, played Actions, Fashion played only for Tips, and every unplayed card still in your hand. Keep legal core Masters and Deep Storage equipped. Refill the Wardrobe Rack to five, then draw a fresh hand of **5 cards**. If your Deck runs out while drawing, shuffle the Archive to form a new Deck and continue. If there still are not enough cards, draw as many as possible. Reset temporary Tips and Appeal.

## Curtain Call

When anyone Slays a Stage, the Stage round ends immediately:

1. award the Stage and its printed Gross SP;
2. every player archives all core Masters, Deep Storage, and Accessories;
3. the active player finishes cleanup and draws back to 5 if possible;
4. if anyone has **30+ Gross SP**, or there is no next Stage, go to Final Judging;
5. otherwise reveal the next Stage. The player clockwise from the Stage claimant becomes First Player.

At Final Judging, apply explicit end-game bonuses and Penalty values. Highest Final Score wins; ties go to most Stage trophies, then fewest Penalties, then highest total printed Tip cost of owned non-Penalty cards, then shared victory.
