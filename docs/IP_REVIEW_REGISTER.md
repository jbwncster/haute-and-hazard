## Aikatsu! visual guardrails

The project may use **general card-design ideas** observed in fashion collectible cards, such as giving the garment visual priority and making category/Brand/performance information easy to scan.

Do not:

- use Aikatsu! character or fashion artwork;
- trace or reproduce official icons;
- copy official logos or Brand marks;
- recreate QR-code placement/treatment;
- reproduce an exact card frame or information-strip arrangement;
- imitate distinctive badges, stars, borders, typography, or decorative compositions closely enough to read as an Aikatsu! card;
- use official card names or proprietary fictional Brands.

The target must read first as **Haute & Hazard**, not as a reskinned card from another property.

See [CARD_VISUAL_REFRESH.md](CARD_VISUAL_REFRESH.md).

## How to add new items

Add a row when:

- a term comes from or strongly resembles another property;
- a logo, illustration, icon, photo, font, or layout uses third-party material;
- a joke, name, character, or venue intentionally references an identifiable person, show, song, brand, or real venue;
- a publisher, lawyer, playtester, or streamer flags an association.

A third-party work cited **only as a documented inspiration source** does not automatically need †. The marker is for an element actually used in Haute & Hazard that still needs rights review.

## Required label text

> † Working prototype term/asset; subject to legal/IP review.

Long form:

> † This prototype term/asset is subject to legal/IP, trademark, rights, and publisher review before commercial release and may be cleared, replaced, renamed, redrawn, or removed.
