## Description

**The lights are up. The Rack is stocked. Can your Queen own the room?**

Haute & Hazard is an original drag-fashion deck-building game about assembling a Coordinate, collecting Tips, shopping for Fashion, and earning Gross SP by Slaying Stages.

### Play solo or together

- **Solo:** take on The House, an automated rival that drafts from the Rack and scores each Stage using its revealed automa card. Choose the House difficulty.
- **Pass and Play:** play with 2–5 people on the same device.

The Godot playtest edition includes all 240 cards, 12 Queens, 12 Stages, and 18 House automa cards from the v7.9 data set. It supports the core Transformation → Reveal → Shopping → Slay/Pass loop.

Some multi-step and conditional card effects require manual resolution. Their rules text remains visible, and the game log calls out effects that need attention. This is a digital playtest build; feedback is welcome.

### Controls

Use the mouse or touchscreen. Choose a Queen and game mode, then select **Play** or **Equip** for cards in your hand. Buttons advance the game through the remaining phases.

### Files

Download the Windows or Linux build for your computer. Extract the zip and run the included executable. The project source is available on GitHub.
