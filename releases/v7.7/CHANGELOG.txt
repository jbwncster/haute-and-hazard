HAUTE & HAZARD v7.7 PLAYTEST UPDATE

- Added a named Judge to every core Stage.
- Added an optional Spotlight Requirement to every core Stage.
- Added a Judge's Favor earned only when the Stage is Slayed while fulfilling
  its Spotlight Requirement.
- Added a Featured Brand to every core Stage.
- Added Brand Ovation: +2 Gross SP when the Stage is Slayed with Face, Wig,
  Body, and Shoes all from that exact Brand.
- Clarified that Judge's Favor and Brand Ovation are independent bonuses.
- Updated the rulebook, glossary Player Aid, card manifest, replacement Stage
  print sheets, teaching game, and Tabletop Simulator saves/assets.
- Added promotional materials and an explicit multiple-test-versions notice.
