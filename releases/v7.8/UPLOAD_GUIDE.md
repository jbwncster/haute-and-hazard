## Safe-zone notes

Game Crafter square-edged card templates use a cut line about 37 px from the edge and a safe zone 75 px from the edge at 300 dpi. Essential text in this package is kept inside that safe area. Do not upload files from `PROOFS_DO_NOT_UPLOAD`.

## Mechanical systems retained

- Tenet/Brand color system.
- Shopping cost economy using Tips.
- Appeal / Matching / Perfect Illusion / Fusion.
- Dragdagulan using printed LS on the three battle-drawn cards plus explicit LS modifiers.

- Each Queen has a once-per-game Special Appeal in addition to the Signature Ability.
