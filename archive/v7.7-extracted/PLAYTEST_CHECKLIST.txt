HAUTE & HAZARD v7.7 - FIRST PLAYTEST CHECKLIST

BEFORE PLAY
[ ] Print and sleeve one 12-card starter deck per player.
[ ] Build the Wardrobe Rack, Thrift Store, Penalty Stack, and 12-card Stage Deck.
[ ] For the teaching game, give Player 1 Siren Diesel and Player 2 Opal Dynasty.
[ ] Give each player a glossary Player Aid and a Player Mat or four labeled slots.
[ ] Prepare counters for Tips, Appeal, and Gross SP.
[ ] Set the Market Row to five cards.

RECORD AFTER EACH GAME
- Player count and total play time
- Winning Queen and Final Score
- Number of Stages Slayed
- Number of Dragdagulan attempts and wins
- Cards or effects that caused a rules question
- Turns with no useful play
- Whether 30 Gross SP felt too fast, too slow, or right
- Whether the Market Row changed often enough to keep Shopping interesting
- Whether the Stage sequence created escalating pressure
- Whether players felt rewarded for improving their personal decks
- How often players fulfilled a Judge's Spotlight Requirement
- How often players completed the exact Featured Brand
- Whether +2 SP felt appropriate for Brand Ovation

RULES PRIORITY
Explicit card override > v7.7 Player Aid > v7.7 Core Rulebook > older printed text.

CORE LOCKS FOR THIS TEST
- Shopping uses Tips and the Market Row / Thrift Store.
- Dragdagulan draws three cards from each player's personal Deck.
- Battle Score is printed LS on those three cards plus explicit modifiers.
- Equipped cards do not add printed LS unless an effect explicitly says so.
- Tenet means Pink, Blue, Purple, or Yellow. Neutral is colorless.
