# HAUTE & HAZARD
## Core Game Rulebook - Version 7.7 PLAYTEST

*Walk the runway. Build the Coordinate. Slay the Stage.*

> **PLAYTEST STATUS**  
> This unified edition aligns the complete core card pool, terminology, player aid,
> and teaching game. It is mechanically playable but not yet balance-certified.

## Gameplay identity

The tabletop engine blends the shared Market Row and personal-deck growth of a
competitive deck-building game with a shared sequence of escalating Stage
challenges. Players improve their own decks, interpret the same Active Stage,
compete for Gross SP, and may confront one another through Dragdagulan.

Every playable Fashion or Action card retains four core numeric fields:
**Cost, Tips, Appeal, and LS**. Haute, Hazard, Camp, and Nerve may appear in
future material only as optional style tags; they do not replace Tenet, Brand,
Cost, Tips, Appeal, or LS in this core set.

---

# 1. Game concept

In **Haute & Hazard**, each player controls a Queen building a fashion Coordinate over the course of a Round. Equip fashion, chain Actions, build a deck from the Wardrobe Rack, reveal Matching and Fusion Looks, and use Appeal to Slay the active Stage.

A successful Stage Slay ends the Round with a **Curtain Call**, clearing every player's equipped Coordinate before the next Stage begins.

The standard game is for **2–5 players**.

## Goal

Earn Gross Style Points (SP) from Stages, Queen abilities, cards, and Dragdagulan. The game moves to Final Judging when:

- at least one player has **30 or more Gross SP** at the end of Phase 4 or a Curtain Call, **or**
- the Stage Deck is empty after a Stage is Slayed.

At Final Judging, apply penalties and explicit end-game bonuses. Highest Final Score wins.

**Optional Short Game:** use 20 Gross SP instead of 30.

# 3. The Player Mat and zones

Each player has the following play areas.

## 3.1 Four Core Slots — the Coordinate

1. **Face**
2. **Wig**
3. **Body**
4. **Shoes**

The top card of an occupied core slot is its **Master**.

A Coordinate is **Complete** when all four core slots have a Master.

Core Fashion persists across turns until replaced, removed by an effect, or cleared by Curtain Call.

## 3.2 Accessory Slot

A player may have one Accessory equipped at a time.

- Accessory is not a core slot.
- It does not count toward Complete, Matching, Perfect Illusion, or Fusion unless a card explicitly says otherwise.
- The Accessory is archived during that player's Cleanup.

## 3.3 Action Row

Actions played during Transformation are placed face-up here after they resolve. They remain visible for the turn and are archived during Cleanup.

## 3.4 Personal deck zones

**Deck** — face-down personal draw pile.

**Hand** — private cards available to play.

**Backstage Archive** — personal discard pile. When the Deck must be drawn from and is empty, shuffle the Archive to make a new Deck.

**Personal Trash** — face-up cards removed from the normal deck cycle. Trashed cards do not reshuffle unless an effect specifically retrieves them.

## 3.5 Shared zones

**Wardrobe Rack** — shared face-down shopping deck plus five-card Market Row.

**Boutique Trash** — shared face-up zone for Wardrobe Rack cards removed from the market by card effects.

**Thrift Store** — finite face-up supply of Thrift Store Throwbacks.

**Penalty Stack** — shared penalty supply.

**Stage Deck** — shared challenge deck and one Active Stage.

# 5. Card types

## 5.1 Fashion

Fashion equips to a legal slot and can remain on the Player Mat.

Base Fashion slots are:

- Face
- Wig
- Body
- Shoes
- Accessory

**Makeup** is treated as a Face subtype; it occupies the Face slot.

Fashion can show:

- Cost
- printed Tips
- printed Appeal
- LS
- Brand/Tenet
- one or more abilities
- continuous keywords

## 5.2 Actions

Actions do not occupy Fashion slots.

During Transformation, play an Action from hand, resolve it completely, then place it in the Action Row.

Unless a card specifically limits you, you may play **any number of Actions** during your Transformation.

## 5.3 Vogue Actions

Vogue is an Action family used for performance chains. It is **not** a Deep Storage keyword in the Base Game.

The standard chain is:

**Hands → Walk → Finish**

- Hands begins the chain.
- Walk played immediately after Hands receives its printed chain benefit.
- Finish played immediately after a qualifying Walk receives its printed chain benefit.
- Playing an unrelated Action breaks the chain.
- A chain ends at Cleanup.

## 5.4 Penalties

Penalty cards can enter a player's Deck/Archive through battle or effects.

Unless a card explicitly says otherwise:

- Penalties have no Tips, Appeal, or LS.
- Penalties cannot be played as Fashion or Actions.
- Penalties cannot be trashed by ordinary trash effects.
- They are still cards in hand and can make a hand less efficient.

Their negative SP is applied during Final Judging.

# 6. Ability timing

A card may have any number of the following ability types. Missing ability types simply do not exist on that card.

## Play

Resolves immediately when:

- a Fashion card is legally equipped from hand/Archive, or
- an Action is played.

A card moved only between Master and Deep Storage without being newly equipped does not trigger Play again unless its text says so.

## Fusion

Resolves once during The Reveal if the controller has a Fusion Look.

## Matching

Resolves once during The Reveal if the controller has a Matching Look.

## Reaction

May interrupt only at the timing written on the card. Resolve the Reaction before continuing the interrupted effect.

## Continuous

Always active while the card is in an eligible zone. `Wild` and `Deep Storage` are continuous keywords.

## Simultaneous triggers

When several of your effects trigger at the same time, choose their order.

If several players have effects at the same timing point, resolve the active player's effects first, then players clockwise from the active player.

Resolve each effect fully before the next effect begins unless a Reaction interrupts it.

## Targets

If an effect says **an opponent** in a multiplayer game, its controller chooses one opponent unless the text says each/all/random opponent.

If no legal target exists, that portion of the effect does nothing.

## Delayed effects

Use a reminder marker for “next turn” effects.

A delayed effect expires after it resolves or after the specified timing passes. Multiple copies stack unless the card says otherwise.

# 7. Master cards and Deep Storage

## 7.1 Master

The top visible Fashion card in each core slot is the **Master**.

Only the Master:

- supplies that slot's visible Tenet for Matching/Perfect Illusion,
- contributes printed Tips and Appeal during Reveal,
- is normally treated as the equipped Fashion in that slot.

## 7.2 Covering a Master

When you equip Fashion to an occupied matching core slot, choose the new card as the new Master unless its Deep Storage rule lets you tuck it beneath instead.

The old Master:

- remains beneath the new Master if the old card has `Deep Storage` and the slot has room for it,
- otherwise goes to the Backstage Archive.

## 7.3 Tucking a new Deep Storage card

When you equip a Fashion card with `Deep Storage` to an occupied legal core slot, you may tuck that new card beneath the current Master instead of making it Master.

Its Play ability still resolves because it was equipped, but it provides no printed Tips or Appeal while buried.

## 7.4 Base Game storage limit

A core slot can contain:

- one Master, and
- at most **one** Deep Storage card beneath it.

If an effect would create a second buried card, choose one buried card to keep and archive the other unless the effect explicitly overrides this limit.

## 7.5 What buried cards do

A Deep Storage card:

- does not contribute printed Tips or Appeal,
- does not automatically contribute printed LS to battles,
- does not count toward Matching or Perfect Illusion,
- can contribute its Tenet to the Fusion color pool,
- can resolve eligible Fusion text,
- remains until replaced/removed or Curtain Call.

If a Deep Storage effect contains a numerical Appeal bonus, follow the printed/errata text. Under the old v6.7 convention, buried cards do not receive a numerical Fusion Appeal bonus unless the corrected card text explicitly says they do.

# 8. Wild

`Wild` is a continuous keyword.

Before Look-state checks during The Reveal, assign each Wild card **one active non-Neutral Tenet** for that Reveal.

The chosen Tenet is used wherever that card is eligible to contribute to Look checks.

- A Wild Master can help Matching, Perfect Illusion, or Fusion.
- A Wild Deep Storage card can help Fusion but not Matching/Perfect.
- The choice lasts until the end of the turn's Reveal resolution.

A card cannot count as two Tenets at once unless its text explicitly says so.

# 9. Setup

## Step 1 — Stage

Shuffle the 12-card Stage Deck and reveal the top Stage as the **Active Stage**.

## Step 2 — Wardrobe Rack

Shuffle the 144-card Wardrobe Rack.

Reveal five cards face-up as the Market Row.

## Step 3 — Thrift Store

Lay the 16 available Throwbacks face-up so players can inspect the available slot choices. The v7.1 playtest print set uses 3 Face, 3 Wig, 4 Body, 3 Shoes, and 3 Accessory Throwbacks.

## Step 4 — Penalties

Sort or shuffle the Penalty supply as desired, but keep the three penalty types identifiable so effects can take the named card.

## Step 5 — Queens

Each player chooses or randomly receives one Queen.

Use the v7.1 Queen text where the printed/current text conflicts with this rulebook.

## Step 6 — Starter decks

Each player takes one 12-card Dressing Room Floor starter deck, shuffles it, and draws 5 cards.

## Step 7 — Resources

Each player begins at:

- 0 Gross SP
- 0 Tips
- 0 Appeal
- empty Personal Trash
- empty Player Mat

## Step 8 — First player

Determine the first player randomly and give them the First Player marker.

Play begins with that player's Transformation.

# 10. The five-phase turn

Every turn uses these phases in order:

1. **Transformation**
2. **The Reveal**
3. **Shopping**
4. **Slay / Dragdagulan / Pass**
5. **Cleanup**

A player cannot return to a completed phase unless an effect explicitly says so.

---

# 11. Phase 1 — Transformation

At the start of Transformation, confirm your temporary Tips and Appeal are 0.

Then take any legal Transformation actions in any order.

## Equip Fashion

You may equip any number of legal Fashion cards from hand, subject to slot rules.

When Fashion is equipped:

1. place it legally as Master or Deep Storage,
2. resolve its Play ability,
3. do **not** yet add its printed Tips/Appeal merely for equipping it.

Printed Fashion stats are counted during The Reveal.

## Play Actions

You may play any number of Actions from hand one at a time.

For each Action:

1. reveal/play it,
2. resolve its text,
3. gain any immediate Tips/Appeal it provides,
4. place it in your Action Row.

You may use cards drawn by an Action during the same Transformation.

## Use Queen abilities

Use any Queen ability whose timing is Transformation/once per turn.

Unless text says otherwise, an “once per turn” ability resets at the beginning of that Queen's next turn.

## Trash effects

If an effect says “Trash 1 card,” move the specified owned card to your Personal Trash.

A card in Personal Trash is no longer in your normal Deck/Archive cycle.

---

# 12. Phase 2 — The Reveal

The Reveal establishes your current performance for the turn.

Resolve these steps in order.

## Step 1 — Validate slot state

Confirm every core slot has at most one Master and one Deep Storage card.

Archive any illegally buried non-Deep-Storage card left by an unresolved old game state.

## Step 2 — Assign Wild Tenets

Choose one active Tenet for each eligible Wild card.

## Step 3 — Add printed Fashion resources

Add the printed Tips and Appeal of:

- each current Master in Face, Wig, Body, and Shoes,
- the current Accessory, if any.

Do not add printed base stats from Deep Storage cards.

These printed resources are added **once per turn**, even if that Fashion has persisted from an earlier turn.

## Step 4 — Determine Look states

First determine whether the Coordinate is **Complete**: all four core slots have Masters.

If incomplete, it cannot be Matching, Perfect Illusion, or Fusion unless an effect explicitly overrides the requirement.

### Matching Look

A Complete Coordinate is Matching if all four visible Masters are the same non-Neutral Tenet.

### Perfect Illusion

A Matching Look is a Perfect Illusion if its Tenet equals the Queen's Signature Tenet.

### Fusion Look

A Complete Coordinate is Fusion if the **Fusion color pool** contains exactly two distinct non-Neutral Tenets.

The Fusion color pool contains:

- the Tenet of every core Master,
- plus the Tenet of each eligible Deep Storage card.

Neutral cards do not add a Tenet to the Fusion pool. If fewer than two or more than two non-Neutral Tenets are present, the Look is not Fusion.

### Simultaneous states

A visible Matching/Perfect Coordinate may also be Fusion if Deep Storage introduces a second Tenet. This is legal.

## Step 5 — Resolve Fusion abilities

If Fusion is active, resolve eligible Fusion abilities on cards you control in the order you choose.

## Step 6 — Resolve Matching abilities

If Matching is active, resolve eligible Matching abilities on cards you control in the order you choose.

Accessories can resolve their own Matching/Fusion abilities even though they do not help establish the Look state.

## Step 7 — Apply the Appeal multiplier

Apply exactly one multiplier to your current Appeal total:

- **Perfect Illusion:** ×3
- otherwise **Matching Look:** ×2
- otherwise: ×1

Perfect Illusion replaces the Matching ×2; the multipliers do not stack.

Round fractional results only if a future effect introduces fractions; by default all values are integers.

## Step 8 — Gain Untouchable

If you revealed Perfect Illusion, gain **Untouchable** until the next Curtain Call or until an effect removes it.

## Step 9 — Lock the Reveal state

Your Matching/Fusion/Perfect status remains the result of this Reveal for effects later in your turn unless an effect explicitly says it recalculates the Coordinate.

Appeal gained **after** Step 7 is added normally and is not multiplied retroactively.

---

# 13. Phase 3 — Shopping

Spend Tips to improve your deck.

## Wardrobe purchases

You may buy any number of Market Row cards you can afford.

For each purchase:

1. pay its printed/modified Tip cost,
2. place the purchased card in your Backstage Archive,
3. refill the empty Market Row space from the Wardrobe Rack if possible.

A cost cannot be reduced below 0 unless an effect explicitly allows a negative cost benefit.

## Thrift Store purchases

You may buy any available Throwback you can afford.

Purchased Throwbacks go to your Backstage Archive.

The Thrift supply is finite. Do not replace a purchased copy unless another physical copy remains.

## Boutique Trash purchases

You may buy from Boutique Trash only if an effect explicitly gives permission. Use the card's printed cost unless the effect says otherwise.

A purchased Boutique Trash card goes to the buyer's Backstage Archive.

## Wardrobe Rack depletion

If the Wardrobe Rack draw pile is empty, do not refill empty Market Row spaces. The game continues.

Wardrobe depletion does not end the game.

## Unspent Tips

At the end of Shopping, unspent Tips remain available until Cleanup for effects that care about Tips, but cannot normally be spent outside Shopping.

They reset to 0 during Cleanup.

# 14. Phase 4 — Slay, Dragdagulan, or Pass

Choose exactly one of the following unless a card says otherwise.

## Option A — Slay the Active Stage

You may declare a Slay if:

- your current Appeal meets or exceeds the Stage's target, and
- every prerequisite is currently satisfied.

If the Stage has a prerequisite cost, pay it as part of declaring the Slay.

### Example — The Filth Floor

“Must Trash 1 card from hand” means you must have an eligible card in hand and trash it when declaring the Slay. If you cannot pay the cost, you cannot Slay that Stage.

### Successful Slay

1. Take the Stage as a trophy.
2. Gain its printed SP on your Gross SP track **once**.
3. Check the Stage's optional Spotlight Requirement.
4. If it was fulfilled, unlock and resolve or retain the printed Judge's Favor.
5. Resolve any other “when you Slay” effects.
6. Perform **Curtain Call**.

Stage trophies do not add their printed SP a second time during Final Judging.

### The Judge and Spotlight Requirement

Every core Stage names a **Judge**. The Judge supplies an optional **Spotlight
Requirement** in addition to the normal Slay Target and mandatory Stage
Prerequisite.

- You never need the Spotlight Requirement merely to Slay the Stage.
- Check it only when the Stage is successfully Slayed.
- If it is fulfilled, the claimant unlocks that Stage's **Judge's Favor**.
- If it is not fulfilled, the claimant still gains the normal Stage reward.
- A Judge's Favor marked “Once per game” can be used once by that Stage's owner.
  Turn the trophy sideways or place a used marker on it after resolving the power.
- A persistent Favor, such as an end-game bonus, remains active as printed.
- A Stage claimed without its Favor cannot unlock that Favor later.

This creates two levels of success: meet the ordinary Slay conditions to advance
the shared Stage sequence, or also satisfy the Judge for an additional strategic
reward.

### Featured Brand and Brand Ovation

Every Stage also names one **Featured Brand**. When a player Slays that Stage,
check whether the four visible core Masters - Face, Wig, Body, and Shoes - all
belong to that exact Brand.

- An Accessory is not required and does not break a Full Brand Coordinate.
- Deep Storage does not count toward the four-card Brand check.
- Wild changes a card's Tenet for the Reveal; it does not change the card's Brand.
- If all four Masters match the Featured Brand, the claimant earns the printed
  **Brand Ovation** in addition to the normal Stage reward.
- The core v7.7 Brand Ovation is **+2 Gross SP** on every Stage for consistent
  balance testing.
- Brand Ovation and Judge's Favor are independent. A claimant may earn neither,
  either one, or both on the same Slay.

The optional Brand reward encourages players to collect recognizable full sets
without making a particular Market Row draw mandatory for normal progress.

## Option B — Dragdagulan

Challenge one legal opponent. Resolve Section 15.

## Option C — Pass

Do neither and proceed to Cleanup.

# 15. Dragdagulan — the Sidewalk Slay-Down

## 15.1 Legal target

Choose one opponent who is not Untouchable.

If no opponent is a legal target, you cannot choose Dragdagulan.

Being Untouchable does not prevent you from initiating Dragdagulan unless an effect says otherwise.

## 15.2 Battle draw

Challenger and defender each draw 3 cards from their personal Deck as **battle cards**.

Battle cards are kept separate from hand and cannot be played.

If a Deck empties while drawing, shuffle that player's Backstage Archive to create a new Deck and continue drawing. If there are still too few cards, draw as many as possible; each missing card contributes LS 0.

## 15.3 Lyric performance

For flavor, players may read the Lyric Snippet of their battle cards in draw order. This has no mechanical effect unless a future card says otherwise.

## 15.4 Battle Score

Each player's Battle Score is:

**LS on their battle-drawn cards + all explicit active “+LS in battle” modifiers**

Important:

- an equipped Master's printed LS does not automatically add to Battle Score,
- Deep Storage LS does not automatically add,
- cards with no printed LS are LS 0,
- penalty cards are LS 0.

## 15.5 Winner

Higher Battle Score wins.

The loser receives **The Chop** from the Penalty supply and places it in their Backstage Archive if a copy is available.

Then the winner chooses one:

1. **Steal 1 Gross SP** — loser loses 1 Gross SP, minimum 0; winner gains 1 Gross SP, or
2. **Wardrobe Malfunction** — loser takes one Wardrobe Malfunction into their Backstage Archive if available.

**Extermination is not a normal winner choice.** It is given only by explicit card/Stage effects.

## 15.6 Tie

If tied:

- both players take The Chop if available,
- no SP is stolen,
- no additional winner reward occurs.

## 15.7 Finish battle

Both players archive all battle-drawn cards.

Then the active player continues to Cleanup.

---

# 16. Phase 5 — Cleanup

If the turn did not already perform Cleanup during Curtain Call:

1. archive your current Accessory,
2. archive all cards in your Action Row,
3. archive your remaining hand,
4. draw 5 cards,
5. reset Tips and Appeal to 0,
6. expire delayed effects that end after this turn,
7. pass play clockwise.

Core Masters and Deep Storage remain equipped.

If the Deck empties while drawing, shuffle the Backstage Archive to create a new Deck and continue.

If you cannot draw five even after reshuffling, draw as many as possible.

---

# 17. Curtain Call — Round end

A successful Stage Slay immediately causes Curtain Call.

## Step 1 — Stage reward

The claimant already took the Stage trophy and gained its printed Gross SP.

## Step 2 — Clear every Coordinate

Every player archives:

- all four core Masters,
- all Deep Storage cards,
- any Accessory.

Clear Untouchable and any effects that last “until Curtain Call.”

## Step 3 — Active player finishes their turn

The active player:

- archives Action Row,
- archives remaining hand,
- draws 5,
- resets Tips and Appeal to 0,
- clears appropriate end-of-turn effects.

Other players keep their current hands and Action Rows do not exist outside their own turns under normal play.

## Step 4 — Check Stage Deck / end trigger

If the Stage Deck has no card to reveal, proceed to Final Judging.

Otherwise reveal the next Active Stage.

If any player has reached the Gross SP end trigger, proceed to Final Judging after Curtain Call is complete.

## Step 5 — Begin the next Round

If the game continues, give the First Player marker to the player clockwise from the Stage claimant.

That player begins the new Round with Transformation.

# 18. Gross SP and Final Judging

## Gross SP

Gross SP is the single live victory-point total tracked during play.

Sources can include:

- Stage rewards
- Queen abilities
- card abilities
- Dragdagulan steals

A Stage trophy is evidence of the Slay but its printed SP is **not counted again** later.

Gross SP cannot go below 0 unless a card explicitly says otherwise.

## End trigger

At the end of Phase 4 or Curtain Call, if any player has at least 30 Gross SP, the game proceeds to Final Judging.

The game also proceeds to Final Judging if the Stage Deck is empty after a Slay.

## Final Score

For each player:

**Final Score = Gross SP + explicit end-game bonuses − Penalty values**

Penalty values:

- Wardrobe Malfunction: −1 SP
- The Chop: −1 SP
- Extermination: −2 SP

Count all Penalties the player owns, regardless of whether they are in Deck, hand, Archive, or another personal zone.

## Winner

Highest Final Score wins.

### Tiebreakers

1. Most Stage trophies
2. Fewest total Penalty cards owned
3. Highest combined printed Tip cost of all owned non-Penalty cards
4. Shared victory

# 20. Current card-text rulings and errata

These rulings are part of v7.0 until corrected cards are produced.

## Chapstick
Treat `Makeup` as **Face**. If the card has no printed LS value, LS 0.

## Wild cards
The following “counts as ANY color / wild for Sets” text is continuous `Wild` for Look checks, not a Fusion-gated ability:

- Anti-Gravity Heels
- Sharpie Brows
- Rainbow Afro
- Clown White
- Living Moss Robe, where applicable
- any other v6.7 card using equivalent wording

## Anti-Gravity Heels
Treat `Wild. Deep Storage.` as continuous keywords.

## Rat Tail Purse
“Retrieve a Trashed card” means choose one eligible card from **your Personal Trash** and return it to hand.

## Cloven Hooves
“You may buy cards from the Trash pile this turn” means you may buy eligible cards from **Boutique Trash** during Shopping at printed cost.

## Shade Fan
Replace its undefined Attack wording with:

> **Reaction:** While Shade Fan is equipped, archive Shade Fan to cancel one hostile Play effect that targets you.

A hostile effect is an opponent-controlled effect that directly chooses you or one of your cards as a target.

## Chrome Face Plate
If its current text says it copies another Face card in play, use:

> **Fusion:** Choose another Face Master in play. Copy that card's Fusion ability until the end of this Reveal. Do not copy its printed stats, Play ability, Matching ability, Brand, Tenet, or keywords.

If no legal Face Master exists, this does nothing.

## Medusa Locks
If its Play effect makes “an opponent” discard, choose one opponent; that player discards one random card from hand.

## Skull Choker
For a “lose 1 Tip next turn” effect, give the target a reminder marker. During that target's next Reveal, after Fashion resources are added, reduce their Tips by 1 to a minimum of 0, then remove the marker.

## Final Girl Bob
If its effect gives an Extermination, choose one opponent and give them one Extermination from the supply if available.

## Butcher's Apron
If its effect requires an opponent to discard an Action, choose one opponent. That opponent chooses one Action in hand and archives it if able.

## Stitched Mouth
The chosen opponent cannot play Action cards during their next Transformation. Remove the reminder marker after that Transformation ends.

## Tentacle Prosthetic
The chosen opponent draws one fewer card at their next Cleanup for each active copy of this delayed effect, to a minimum hand draw of 0; then remove the markers.

## Event Horizon Dress
If its Matching ability grants Untouchable, that status lasts until the next Curtain Call unless the card explicitly gives a shorter duration.

## Pageant Updo
“Immune to Discard” means:

> While Pageant Updo is your Wig Master, opponent-controlled effects cannot force you to discard cards from hand.

It does not protect against Trash, Archive costs you choose to pay, Cleanup, or battle-card archiving.

## Sequined Gown
When a copy successfully resolves its Matching end-game condition for the first time, place a **Crown marker** on that physical card.

A Crowned Sequined Gown grants its stated 5 SP end-game bonus once at Final Judging. A copy cannot gain more than one Crown marker.

## Diamond Scepter
If its Matching text gives a bonus for a Stage Slay, mark the Scepter when its Matching ability resolves. If its controller Slays the Stage during that same turn, gain the stated bonus SP, then clear the marker.


## Bog Root Corset
The old phrase “Camp card” is undefined in the v6.7 rules. In v7.1, treat it as:

> **Play:** Return 1 Yellow card from your Backstage Archive to hand.

## Vogue chain
For current Wardrobe Actions:

- **Catwalk Strut (Walk):** if played immediately after Hands, resolve its chain bonus.
- **Spin & Dip (Finish):** if played immediately after a qualifying Walk, gain its printed chain bonus in addition to normal Action stats.

# 22. Penalty supply edge cases

If an effect instructs a player to take a named Penalty and no copy remains in the supply, that portion of the effect does nothing.

Do not substitute a stronger or weaker Penalty unless the effect explicitly permits a choice.

Penalty cards owned by players remain part of their deck cycle unless an effect explicitly removes them.

# 23. Rules precedence

When rules conflict, use this order:

1. an explicit card effect that says it overrides a normal rule
2. this v7.0 Base Rulebook
3. v7.0 Card Errata
4. printed/current v6.7 card text
5. older v6.7/v3.1 documents only as historical reference

If an old rule refers to Torso, Hips, five core slots, six active Tenets, Instant Slay, Attack, Defense, or “Grand Finale” as a round-end procedure, use the corrected v7.0 rule instead.

# 24. Quick reference

## Turn

1. **Transformation** — equip Fashion; play Actions; resolve Play effects
2. **Reveal** — add Master/Accessory stats; determine Looks; resolve Fusion/Matching; multiply Appeal
3. **Shopping** — spend Tips; purchases go to Archive
4. **Slay / Dragdagulan / Pass**
5. **Cleanup** — archive Accessory, Action Row, hand; draw 5; reset resources

## Coordinate

Core = **Face + Wig + Body + Shoes**

Accessory is temporary and not core.

## Look states

**Complete:** all 4 core Masters present

**Matching:** Complete + all visible Masters same non-Neutral Tenet → ×2 Appeal

**Perfect Illusion:** Matching + Queen Signature Tenet → ×3 Appeal + Untouchable

**Fusion:** Complete + exactly 2 non-Neutral Tenets among Masters + eligible Deep Storage

Matching and Fusion may coexist.

## Resources

- Fashion printed Tips/Appeal score once during Reveal.
- Actions/Play effects can add resources immediately.
- Deep Storage base stats = 0.
- Tips/Appeal reset at Cleanup.
- Appeal added after the Reveal multiplier is not multiplied.

## Slay

Meet Stage Appeal + prerequisite in Phase 4 → claim trophy + gain SP once → Curtain Call.

## Dragdagulan

Each draws 3 battle cards → sum printed LS + explicit battle modifiers.

Winner: loser gets Chop; winner steals 1 SP **or** gives Wardrobe Malfunction.

Tie: both get Chop.

## End game

30 Gross SP trigger or Stage Deck exhausted → Final Judging.

**Final Score = Gross SP + end-game bonuses − Penalties.**

# 23. Glossary

**Active Stage:** The shared challenge currently available to Slay.

**Appeal:** Your performance total for the current turn. It resets during Cleanup.

**Archive:** Put a card into its owner's Backstage Archive, the personal discard pile.

**Backstage Archive:** A player's personal discard pile. It is reshuffled when that player's Deck runs out.

**Brand:** A named fashion line inside a Tenet, such as Trash Can or Velvet Trap.

**Brand Ovation:** The bonus earned by Slaying with all four visible core Masters from the Stage's Featured Brand. In core v7.7, gain 2 Gross SP.

**Complete Coordinate:** Face, Wig, Body, and Shoes each have a Master.

**Coordinate:** The Fashion cards equipped in your Face, Wig, Body, Shoes, and optional Accessory slots.

**Curtain Call:** The Round-ending cleanup after a Stage is Slayed. Every player archives their equipped Coordinate.

**Deep Storage:** One eligible buried Fashion card beneath a Master. It can help Fusion but does not normally provide printed Tips, Appeal, or LS.

**Dragdagulan:** A head-to-head LS battle. Each participant draws three battle cards from their personal Deck.

**Fusion Look:** A Complete Coordinate whose eligible Masters and Deep Storage contain exactly two non-Neutral Tenets.

**Featured Brand:** The exact Brand named by the Active Stage for its optional Brand Ovation.

**Gross SP:** Persistent victory points. Highest Final Score wins.

**Judge:** The persona evaluating a Stage and offering its optional bonus objective.

**Judge's Favor:** A power unlocked when the Stage claimant also fulfills its Spotlight Requirement. Most Favors are usable once per game.

**LS:** Lip Sync strength. Only LS printed on the three battle-drawn cards plus explicit modifiers counts in Dragdagulan.

**Market Row:** Five face-up Wardrobe cards available to buy with Tips.

**Master:** The visible top Fashion card in a core slot.

**Matching Look:** A Complete Coordinate whose four visible Masters share one non-Neutral Tenet.

**Perfect Illusion:** A Matching Look whose Tenet matches the Queen's Signature Tenet. Appeal is tripled and the Queen becomes Untouchable.

**Personal Trash:** Cards removed from a player's normal deck cycle.

**Slay:** Claim the Active Stage by meeting its Appeal target and prerequisites.

**Spotlight Requirement:** An optional extra condition checked only when a Stage is Slayed. It is not required to claim the Stage.

**Tenet:** A core gameplay color: Pink, Blue, Purple, or Yellow. Neutral is colorless.

**Thrift Store:** A finite face-up supply of Throwback cards that may be bought during Shopping.

**Tips:** Shopping currency for the current turn. It resets during Cleanup.

**Untouchable:** Cannot be chosen as the target of Dragdagulan.

**Wild:** Assign the card one non-Neutral Tenet for the current Reveal.

# 24. Scripted First Game

Use this two-player teaching game before shuffling everything normally.

## Recommended first-time Queens

**Player 1 - Siren Diesel (Yellow / Trash Can):** Her ability triggers whenever she equips Shoes as Master and immediately teaches the connection between Transformation and Shopping.

**Player 2 - Opal Dynasty (Purple / Velvet Trap):** Her ability rewards the first Complete Coordinate each Round and teaches players to fill all four core slots.

Avoid Lady Lux, Mother Mortis, Hexa Laroux, and Gore-Jess for the first teaching game; their timing, recursion, economy loops, or battle focus require more system knowledge.

## Teaching setup

1. Use **Face Face Face** as the first Active Stage: Target 8 Appeal, +4 Gross SP, prerequisite Face Master. Madame Visage's optional Spotlight Requirement asks for a Face Master with at least 2 printed Appeal.
2. Each player uses the normal 12-card starter Deck: 7 Basic Beat, 3 Messy Lip Sync, 2 Chapstick.
3. Place these cards in the opening Market Row: Duct Tape Boots, Sharpie Brows, Vamp Lip, Feather Boa, and Silk Stockings.
4. Put Spaghetti Wig, Bin Bag Gown, Femme Fatale Waves, and Velvet Corset on top of the Wardrobe Rack in that order for refills.
5. Siren Diesel takes the first turn.
6. For this teaching game only, arrange starter draws so the active player can follow the examples below. After the example, return to normal shuffled play.

## Example Turn 1 - Siren Diesel

Siren's hand contains 3 Basic Beat, 1 Messy Lip Sync, and 1 Chapstick.

1. **Transformation:** Play the three Basic Beat Actions for 3 Tips. Play Messy Lip Sync for 1 Appeal. Keep Chapstick in hand.
2. **Reveal:** Siren has no equipped Fashion, so there are no Fashion stats or Look bonuses. Final Appeal is 1.
3. **Shopping:** Spend 3 Tips to buy Duct Tape Boots. Put them into the Backstage Archive. Refill the Market Row with Spaghetti Wig.
4. **Phase 4:** Siren cannot Slay Target 8 and chooses Pass.
5. **Cleanup:** Archive played Actions and the remaining hand, draw 5, and reset Tips and Appeal.

## Example Turn 1 - Opal Dynasty

Opal's hand contains 4 Basic Beat and 1 Messy Lip Sync.

1. Play the four Basic Beat Actions for 4 Tips and Messy Lip Sync for 1 Appeal.
2. Buy Vamp Lip for 4 Tips. It enters the Backstage Archive. Refill with Bin Bag Gown.
3. Opal cannot Slay and Passes, then completes Cleanup.

The first cycle demonstrates the deck-builder loop: play starter cards, spend Tips in the shared Market Row, and place purchases in the Archive so they enter a later reshuffle.

## Example Turn 3 - Queen abilities appear

After each player has reshuffled once, arrange the new cards into their next example hand.

**Siren:** Equip Duct Tape Boots as Shoes Master. Printed stats are Cost 3, Tips 0, Appeal 1, LS 1. Built to Walk immediately gives 1 Tip. During The Reveal, the Boots add 1 Appeal. The printed LS does not add to Appeal and does not automatically count in Dragdagulan.

**Opal:** Equip Vamp Lip as Face Master. During The Reveal it adds 2 Appeal. Opal is not Complete yet, so Full Spectrum does not trigger.

## Building toward a Slay

Over later turns, Siren buys and equips Sharpie Brows, Spaghetti Wig, and Bin Bag Gown. Her four Masters are all Yellow:

- Sharpie Brows: Face, 0 Appeal
- Spaghetti Wig: Wig, 1 Appeal
- Bin Bag Gown: Body, 2 Appeal
- Duct Tape Boots: Shoes, 1 Appeal

The printed Fashion Appeal is 4. The Coordinate is Complete and Matching, and Yellow matches Siren's Signature Tenet, so it is a Perfect Illusion. Multiply 4 Appeal by 3 for 12 Appeal. Siren meets Face Face Face's Target 8 and Face prerequisite, Slays it, and gains 4 Gross SP. Sharpie Brows has 0 printed Appeal, so Siren does not meet Madame Visage's Spotlight Requirement and does not unlock Painted for the Gods. Her full outfit is Trash Can, while the Stage's Featured Brand is Big Top, so she also does not earn the +2 SP Brand Ovation. This demonstrates that both bonus objectives are optional: the Stage is still Slayed normally. Curtain Call then archives every player's equipped Coordinate and reveals the next Stage.

## Opal demonstrates Complete without Matching

On a later Stage, Opal equips four Masters that include at least two Tenets. The Coordinate is Complete but not Matching. Full Spectrum triggers once that Round and gives 2 Gross SP. Printed Appeal is added normally with no Matching multiplier. This shows that a Queen ability can reward completion even when the Look is not Perfect.

## Sample Dragdagulan

On a turn when neither Queen is Untouchable, Opal chooses Dragdagulan instead of Slay or Pass.

- Siren's three battle cards show LS 1, LS 2, and LS 0: Battle Score 3.
- Opal's three battle cards show LS 2, LS 2, and LS 0. An explicit active ability gives +1 LS: Battle Score 5.

Opal wins. Siren takes The Chop into her Backstage Archive. Opal chooses either to steal 1 Gross SP or give Wardrobe Malfunction. Both players archive the three battle cards. Equipped Masters contribute no printed LS unless an effect explicitly says they do.

## End the teaching game

Stop after both players have bought a card, equipped Fashion, completed a Coordinate, resolved one Stage Slay, and completed one Dragdagulan. Then shuffle normally and begin a full game. For a short first game, use 20 Gross SP; for the standard game, use 30 Gross SP.
