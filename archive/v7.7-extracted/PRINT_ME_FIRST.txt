GAMEPLAY DNA

Haute & Hazard combines the shared-row deck building of DC Deck-Building Game
with the escalating shared challenges of Marvel Legendary. Its fashion fantasy
and performance presentation are inspired by transformation/idol card games,
but its tabletop engine is a competitive deck builder:
- build a personal deck from a shared Market Row;
- improve deck efficiency through purchases, archiving, and trashing;
- confront a shared sequence of Stage challenges;
- compete for Gross SP and use Dragdagulan as direct player interaction.
