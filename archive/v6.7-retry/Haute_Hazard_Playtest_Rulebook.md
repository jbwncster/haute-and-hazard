# Haute & Hazard — Playtest Rulebook

Based on the v6.7 base rules and v6.7 original-value card manifest. This edition reorganizes the learning path and keeps the complete source rules below for reference. The cards are a text-first prototype for playtesting.

## At a glance

Build a Coordinate from fashion cards, earn Tips to buy better cards, and use Appeal to claim the active Stage for Style Points (SP). A Stage claim starts a Grand Finale: everyone clears their equipped cards. First to the SP target for the player count wins.

## Setup

1. Shuffle the 12 Stages; reveal one active Stage.
2. Shuffle the Wardrobe Rack and reveal five cards for the Market Row. Put the 16 Throwbacks beside it.
3. Each player chooses a Queen, shuffles a starter deck of seven Basic Beats, three Messy Lip Syncs, and two Chapsticks, then draws five.
4. Set out penalties and tokens. Randomly choose the first player for the opening round.

## Your turn: five phases

1. **Transformation.** Equip cards from your hand, resolve their Play text, and collect printed Tips and Appeal. Covering a slot archives the old card unless it has Deep Storage. Check for Instant Slay after each play.
2. **Reveal.** Count the colors of the top cards in the core slots. Resolve Fusion or Matching abilities and calculate the Appeal multiplier. Check for Instant Slay again.
3. **Shopping.** Spend Tips on any affordable Market Row cards or Throwbacks. Put purchases in your discard pile. Replace each bought Market Row card immediately. Unspent Tips expire.
4. **Slay, battle, or pass.** Claim the Stage if you meet its Appeal and other requirements; otherwise you may challenge another player to Dragdagulan, or pass.
5. **Cleanup.** Discard your accessory and remaining hand. Keep equipped core cards until the Grand Finale. Draw five, reshuffling your discard pile when needed; reset turn resources.

## Fast rules

- **Instant Slay:** A qualifying Stage claim in phases 1 or 2 ends the round immediately and skips remaining phases. Check all printed Stage prerequisites too.
- **Grand Finale:** The Stage winner keeps the Stage. Everyone archives cards on their mats; hands stay put. Reveal the next Stage. The active player's skipped turn does not resume after an Instant Slay.
- **Matching:** All five core top cards share a color: double Appeal. If that color is your Queen's signature Tenet: triple Appeal and Untouchable status instead.
- **Fusion:** Exactly two colors among the top core cards enables Fusion abilities. Accessories do not count toward color checks.
- **Battle:** Both players draw three cards, perform their lyric snippets, and compare LS Points. Consult the detailed battle section below for ties and penalties.

## Decisions to settle before a complete playtest

The source documents disagree on these points; no card values have been changed to conceal the conflicts.

1. **Slots:** The rulebook names Face, Wig, Torso, Hips, Shoes, and Accessory, but every wardrobe outfit has a single Body slot and no Hips card. A five-card Matching Look is therefore impossible with the printed manifest as written. Decide whether Body covers both Torso and Hips or create Hips cards, then update the multiplier and Stage prerequisites accordingly.
2. **Instant Slay:** One passage says it is optional; the detailed Stage section says it must be declared. This play guide uses mandatory declaration, matching the detailed section.
3. **Grand Finale timing:** The detailed section says to resume an interrupted player's Phase 5, though Instant Slay skips that phase. Use the explicit Instant Slay skip for the active player; other hands remain unchanged.
4. **Manifest count:** The source calls the Wardrobe Rack 120 cards, yet the printed rows include Vogue cards associated with the Advanced Circuit. Confirm which rows belong in the base deck.
5. **Card text:** Several effects refer to unprovided definitions, including Trashed-card retrieval, Sets, and a Next Turn Tip loss. Resolve these before competitive testing.

## 1. OVERVIEW & GAME CONCEPT

### The Elevator Pitch

In Haute & Hazard, you are not just wearing clothes—you are building a tactical Coordinate. Start from the dressing room floor, scavenge the bins, and curate high-octane Coords to dominate the sidewalk. This is where the card-based fashion of idol anime (Aikatsu style) meets the cutthroat strategy of a deck-builder, all wrapped in the fierce, unapologetic world of drag.

### Game Summary

Players take on the roles of drag queens competing in fashion challenges by building powerful Coordinates (Coords) on their Player Mats. Each turn, you equip fashion items to five core slots plus one accessory slot, generating Tips (currency) and Appeal (performance power). Spend Tips to purchase better cards from the Wardrobe Rack, then use your accumulated Appeal to Slay Stages and earn Style Points. The first player to reach the target SP wins the crown.

### Core Mechanics

**Deck-Building:** Purchase cards from a shared marketplace to improve your personal deck over time, removing weak starter cards and acquiring powerful designer items.

**Tableau Management:** Equip cards to your Player Mat's six slots (Face, Wig, Torso, Hips, Shoes, Accessory), creating a persistent Coordinate that generates resources each turn.

**Brand Synergies:** Match colors across your five core slots for multiplier bonuses. All one color doubles your Appeal. Matching your Queen's signature color triples it and grants immunity to battles.

**Stacking Strategy:** Cards in the same slot stack vertically. The top card (Master) determines color for multipliers, while buried cards with Deep Storage keyword remain hidden to provide secondary effects.

**Round Structure:** Rounds end when any player successfully Slays a Stage, causing all players to discard their equipped Coordinates and start fresh with new hands.

## 2. COMPONENTS LIST

### Card Decks

**The Wardrobe Rack** (120 cards)
The main deck containing fashion items across all six Tenets (brands). Each card has LS Points (0-5) used in battles and provides Tips/Appeal when equipped.

**The Dressing Room Floor** (60 cards)
Five identical 12-card starter decks, one per player. Contains basic neutral cards that generate minimal resources but allow initial gameplay.

**Thrift Store Throwbacks** (16 cards)
Permanent neutral stack cards that provide +2★ Appeal with LS: 0. Available for purchase throughout the game as backup options.

**Penalty Stack** (20 cards)
Junk cards given as penalties for losing battles:
- Wardrobe Malfunctions (-1 SP)
- The Chop (-1 SP)
- Exterminations (-2 SP)

**Stage Deck** (12 cards)
Challenge cards that set Appeal requirements and award Style Points when claimed.

### Player Components

**Player Mats** (5)
Each mat contains six equipment slots:
- Five Core Slots: Face, Wig, Torso, Hips, Shoes (the Coordinate)
- One Bonus Slot: Accessory (does not count toward Coord matching)

**Queen Cards** (11)
Character cards with unique passive or active abilities that define each player's strategic approach.

### Tokens

- Style Point (SP) tokens for tracking victory points
- Tip ($) tokens for purchasing currency
- First Player marker

## 3. SETUP INSTRUCTIONS

Follow these steps in order to prepare the game:

### Step 1: The Runway
Shuffle the Stage Deck and place it face-down in the center of the play area. Flip the top card face-up to reveal the Active Stage. This card shows the Appeal requirement and SP reward for the current round.

### Step 2: The Wardrobe Rack
Shuffle the Wardrobe Rack deck (120 main cards) thoroughly and place it face-down. Deal five cards face-up in a row next to the deck. This is the Market Row where players will shop for fashion items.

### Step 3: The Thrift Store
Place the 16 Thrift Store Throwback cards in a face-up stack near the Wardrobe Rack. These neutral cards remain available for purchase throughout the game.

### Step 4: The Haus
Each player chooses one Queen card and takes the corresponding Dressing Room Floor starter deck (12 cards). Place your Queen face-up in front of you and shuffle your starter deck. Draw five cards to form your opening hand.

### Step 5: Token Pool
Place all Style Point tokens and Tip tokens in the center of the play area within reach of all players.

### Step 6: Underdog's Edge
The player with the lowest current Style Points goes first. On the first turn of the game, determine randomly. Give that player the First Player marker.

Setup is complete. The first player begins their Phase 1: Transformation.

## 4. TURN STRUCTURE

Each player's turn consists of five distinct phases executed in order. You cannot skip phases or return to previous phases.

### PHASE 1: TRANSFORMATION

**Overview:** Play cards from your hand to your Player Mat slots to build your Coordinate.

**Steps:**

1. **Equip Cards:** Play any number of cards from your hand to your Player Mat. You may equip cards to any of the six slots: Face, Wig, Torso, Hips, Shoes, or Accessory.

2. **Slot Replacement:** If a slot already contains a card, you may play a new card on top of it (creating a stack) or replace it entirely. Replaced cards are immediately archived (discarded) unless they have the Deep Storage keyword.

3. **Stacking Rules:** Cards with Deep Storage keyword remain buried under the new card (Master). Cards without this keyword are archived when covered.

4. **Immediate Harvest:** As each card is played, immediately gain any Tips ($) and Appeal (★) listed on the card. These resources accumulate throughout Phase 1.

5. **Check for Instant Slay:** If your total Appeal meets or exceeds the Active Stage's requirement at any point during this phase, you may declare an Instant Slay. This immediately claims the Stage, ends the round for all players, and skips your remaining phases.

**Important Notes:**
- The five core slots (Face, Wig, Torso, Hips, Shoes) form your Coordinate and persist until the round ends
- Accessories are temporary bonus cards that will be archived at the end of your turn
- You are not required to fill all slots—strategic empty slots are permitted

**Example:**
You have a Pink Sugar Rush Wig (Master) in your Wig slot. You play a Blue Necropolis Wig with Deep Storage keyword on top. The Pink wig remains buried beneath the Blue wig. The Blue wig becomes the new Master, so your Wig slot now counts as Blue for brand matching purposes, but the Pink wig continues to provide its buried effects.

---

### PHASE 2: THE REVEAL

**Overview:** Calculate your final resources, apply multipliers, and trigger conditional abilities based on your Coordinate composition.

**Steps:**

1. **Deep Storage Check:** Identify all cards currently buried in slots. Any card with the Deep Storage keyword stays buried and active. All other covered cards are immediately archived.

2. **Count Brand Colors:** Examine the Master card in each of your five core slots (Face, Wig, Torso, Hips, Shoes). Count how many different brand colors are present. Ignore the Accessory slot for this calculation.

3. **Fusion Check:** If your core slots contain exactly two different brand colors, you have achieved a Fusion Look. Activate all "Fusion" abilities listed in the Triple Threat Box of every card currently on your mat.

**Fusion Appeal Rules:**
- If the card with a Fusion ability is a Master card, it grants both the Appeal (★) value and any text effects
- If the card with a Fusion ability is in Deep Storage, it grants only text effects (0★ Appeal)
- Example: A Deep Storage card says "Fusion: +3★". This grants 0★ (because Deep Storage), but if it said "Fusion: Draw 1 card", you would draw the card

4. **Apply Multipliers:** Calculate your total Appeal multiplier based on brand matching:

**Matching Brand:** All five core slots share one brand color → multiply total Appeal × 2

**Perfect Illusion:** All five core slots match your Queen's Signature Tenet → multiply total Appeal × 3 AND gain Untouchable status (cannot be challenged to battles this turn)

**No Multiplier:** Three or more brand colors, or zero-to-four slots filled → Appeal is calculated normally (×1)

5. **Activate Reveal Abilities:** Trigger any abilities on your cards that activate "during Phase 2" or "during The Reveal".

**Important Notes:**
- Only Master cards count for determining brand colors
- Deep Storage cards provide their brand color for Fusion checks but do not count for Matching/Perfect Illusion
- Accessories never count toward brand calculations
- Multipliers apply to your total Appeal after all cards and abilities are resolved

**Example:**
Your core slots contain: Blue Wig (Master), Blue Face (Master), Blue Torso (Master), Blue Hips (Master), Blue Shoes (Master). Your base Appeal from all cards totals 12★. Since all five core slots share the Blue brand, you have Matching Brand. Your final Appeal is 12★ × 2 = 24★.

---

### PHASE 3: SHOPPING

**Overview:** Spend your accumulated Tips to purchase cards from the Wardrobe Rack or Thrift Store.

**Steps:**

1. **Review Available Cards:** Look at the five face-up cards in the Market Row (Wardrobe Rack) and the face-up Thrift Store Throwbacks stack.

2. **Make Purchases:** You may buy any number of cards you can afford. Each card shows its cost in Tips ($) in the upper corner. Pay the cost and take the card.

3. **Purchased Cards Destination:** All purchased cards go directly to your Backstage Archive (discard pile), not to your hand. You will draw them on future turns.

4. **Replace Market Cards:** After each purchase from the Market Row, immediately draw a new card from the Wardrobe Rack deck to keep five cards face-up.

5. **Thrift Store Note:** Purchasing from the Thrift Store does not require replacement since all 16 cards are available throughout the game.

6. **Unspent Tips:** Any Tips you do not spend are lost. Tips do not carry over between turns.

**Shopping Strategy Tips:**
- Higher cost cards generally provide better Appeal values and more powerful abilities
- Look for cards that match your Queen's signature brand for Perfect Illusion potential
- Consider purchasing cards with Deep Storage to build multi-layered slot strategies
- Balance immediate power (high Appeal) with engine-building (card draw, Tip generation)

**Example:**
You have 7 Tips. The Market Row shows: Sugar Rush Wig (3 Tips), Necropolis Torso (4 Tips), Velvet Trap Face (5 Tips), Trash Can Shoes (2 Tips), and Gilded Cage Accessory (6 Tips). You purchase the Necropolis Torso (4 Tips) and Trash Can Shoes (2 Tips), spending 6 Tips total. You have 1 Tip remaining, which is lost. Both purchased cards go to your discard pile.

---

### PHASE 4: SLAY OR BATTLE (THE CHOICE)

**Overview:** Attempt to claim the Active Stage or challenge another player to combat.

You have three options during this phase:

**OPTION A: THE SLAY**

1. **Check Requirements:** Compare your total Appeal (★) from Phase 2 to the Active Stage's requirement shown on the Stage card.

2. **Claim the Stage:** If your Appeal meets or exceeds the requirement, you successfully Slay the Stage. Take the Stage card and add it to your score pile. Gain the Style Points listed on the card.

3. **Trigger Grand Finale:** Slaying a Stage immediately triggers the Grand Finale (Round End) for all players. See Grand Finale rules in Section 8.

4. **New Stage:** After the Grand Finale cleanup, flip the next Stage card face-up to become the new Active Stage.

**OPTION B: DRAGDAGULAN (BATTLE)**

Challenge another player to a Sidewalk Slay-Down. See detailed combat rules in Section 7: Dragdagulan.

**Quick Battle Summary:**
- Both players draw three cards
- Read aloud the Lyric Snippets on cards in order drawn
- Compare total LS Points on the three cards
- Winner steals 1 SP or gives a penalty card to loser
- Loser receives The Chop (-1 SP penalty card)

**OPTION C: PASS**

You may choose to do neither. Your turn proceeds to Phase 5: Cleanup.

**Important Notes:**
- You can only Slay if you have enough Appeal AND meet any prerequisite conditions listed on the Stage
- You can only battle if you are not Untouchable and your target is not Untouchable
- If you Instant Slayed during Phase 1 or 2, you skip this phase entirely

---

### PHASE 5: CLEANUP (INDIVIDUAL TURN END)

**Overview:** Archive temporary cards and refresh your hand for the next turn.

**Steps:**

1. **Archive Accessories:** Discard any cards in your Accessory slot to your Backstage Archive. The Accessory slot is now empty.

2. **Archive Hand:** Discard your entire hand to your Backstage Archive.

3. **Core Slots Persist:** Your five core slots (Face, Wig, Torso, Hips, Shoes) remain face-up on your mat. Do NOT discard them. They persist until the Grand Finale.

4. **Draw New Hand:** Draw five cards from your personal deck. If your deck is empty, shuffle your Backstage Archive to create a new deck and continue drawing.

5. **Reset Resources:** Your Tips ($) and Appeal (★) totals reset to zero. You will recalculate them on your next turn.

6. **Pass Turn:** The next player clockwise begins their Phase 1: Transformation.

**Important Notes:**
- Only Accessories and your hand are discarded during individual turn cleanup
- Core slots remain equipped until someone Slays a Stage (triggering Grand Finale)
- If you cannot draw five cards even after reshuffling, draw as many as possible

**Example:**
You have a Wig, Torso, and Shoes equipped in your core slots, plus an Accessory. You discard the Accessory and your hand of three cards (five total cards discarded). The three core slot cards stay on your mat. You draw five new cards from your deck.

## 5. CARD TYPES & ANATOMY

### Fashion Items (Equipment Cards)

Fashion items are the primary cards in Haute & Hazard. They equip to your Player Mat slots and provide resources.

**Card Anatomy:**

```
┌─────────────────────────────────┐
│ CARD NAME              Cost: $5 │
│ [Brand Color]      [Slot Type]  │
├─────────────────────────────────┤
│                                 │
│        [ARTWORK AREA]           │
│                                 │
├─────────────────────────────────┤
│ Tips: $2  |  Appeal: ★3         │
│ LS Points: 3                    │
├─────────────────────────────────┤
│ TRIPLE THREAT BOX:              │
│ • Play: [Immediate effect]      │
│ • Matching: [If all one brand]  │
│ • Fusion: [If exactly 2 brands] │
├─────────────────────────────────┤
│ "Lyric snippet for battles..."  │
│ [Flavor text]                   │
└─────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Key Elements:**

**Cost:** Tips ($) required to purchase from Wardrobe Rack

**Brand:** One of six Tenets (colors) that determine synergies:
- 💗 Pink (Sugar Rush, Hyper-Glitch)
- ♠️ Blue (Necropolis, Slasher, Void)
- 💎 Purple (Velvet Trap, Gilded Cage)
- ⭐ Yellow (Trash Can, Big Top, Swamp Witch)
- 🟡 Gold (Wealth/Luxury items)
- ⬜ Neutral (starter cards, Thrift Store)

**Slot Type:** Which mat position the card occupies (Face, Wig, Torso, Hips, Shoes, Accessory)

**Tips ($):** Currency generated when played to mat

**Appeal (★):** Performance power used to Slay Stages

**LS Points:** Battle value (0-5) used in Dragdagulan combat

**Triple Threat Box:** Three conditional abilities:
- **Play:** Activates immediately when equipped (Phase 1)
- **Matching:** Activates if you have Matching Brand (Phase 2)
- **Fusion:** Activates if you have Fusion Look (Phase 2)

**Lyric Snippet:** Text read aloud during battles for performance flavor

**Flavor Text:** Thematic description (no mechanical effect)

---

### Special Keywords

**Deep Storage**
Cards with this keyword can remain buried under Master cards in the same slot. They continue to provide their brand color for Fusion checks and activate Fusion abilities, but they cannot contribute numerical Appeal (★) values via Fusion triggers. They persist in Deep Storage until archived by game effects or round end.

Example: "Deep Storage: This card remains active when buried beneath a Master card."

---

### Card Types by Function

**Engine Cards:** Provide card draw or Tip generation to accelerate your deck
Example: "Play: Draw 1 card"

**Power Cards:** Provide high Appeal (★) values to Slay Stages
Example: "Appeal: ★6"

**Utility Cards:** Provide trash mechanics, sabotage, or special timing effects
Example: "Play: Trash 1 card from your hand"

**Deep Storage Cards:** Build multi-layer strategies in single slots
Example: "Deep Storage. Fusion: +2★"

## 6. STACKING LOGIC: MASTER VS DEEP STORAGE

### The Master Card

The Master is the top card in any occupied slot. Only the Master card's brand color counts for Matching Brand or Perfect Illusion calculations. The Master provides its full numerical values (Tips, Appeal, LS Points) and activates all relevant abilities.

**Master Fusion Triggers:** When a Fusion Look is active, Master cards activate their Fusion abilities and provide full Appeal (★) values listed in those abilities.

Example: Master card says "Fusion: +3★". With Fusion active, this grants +3★ Appeal.

---

### Deep Storage Cards

Cards with the Deep Storage keyword remain buried beneath the Master when covered. They are not archived during Phase 2 cleanup like normal cards.

**Deep Storage Fusion Triggers:** Deep Storage cards contribute their brand color for determining Fusion status (exactly 2 colors) and activate their Fusion ability text, but they CANNOT provide numerical Appeal (★) values.

Example: Deep Storage card says "Fusion: +3★". With Fusion active, this grants 0★ (Deep Storage rule) even though the ability triggered.

Example: Deep Storage card says "Fusion: Draw 1 card". With Fusion active, you draw 1 card (text effects work normally).

---

### Stacking Strategy

**Single Layer:** One Master card per slot. Simple and straightforward. Brand color is obvious.

**Two Layer:** Master on top, Deep Storage buried. The Master determines slot color for multipliers. The buried card provides additional Fusion utility without increasing Appeal contribution.

**Strategic Depth:** Deep Storage allows you to "bank" cards in slots for future utility while keeping your current brand color composition optimal for Matching/Perfect Illusion multipliers.

**Example Scenario:**
You have a Pink Wig (Master) and want to maintain Pink Matching Brand across all five slots. You draw a powerful Blue Wig with "Fusion: Draw 2 cards" and Deep Storage keyword. You play it under your Pink Wig. Your slot still counts as Pink (Master determines color), but when you achieve Fusion Look on a future turn, the buried Blue wig triggers its draw effect.

## 7. DRAGDAGULAN: THE SIDEWALK SLAY-DOWN

Dragdagulan is the technical term for the battle system in Haute & Hazard. When you choose to battle during Phase 4, follow these steps:

### Step 1: Declaration
The active player announces their target. The target must accept the challenge unless they have Untouchable status (granted by Perfect Illusion or other card effects).

### Step 2: The Draw
Both the challenger and defender draw three cards from their personal decks. Keep these cards separate from your regular hand.

### Step 3: The Lyric Rule
Starting with the challenger, both players read aloud the Lyric Snippet on each of their three cards in the order drawn. This creates a back-and-forth performance with alternating lines from each player's cards.

This is purely for flavor and entertainment—no mechanical effect.

### Step 4: Calculate LS Points
Each player adds up the LS Points (0-5) shown on their three battle cards. The player with the higher total wins the battle. In case of a tie, both players lose (see consequences below).

### Step 5: Battle Consequences

**Winner's Choice (pick one):**
- Steal 1 Style Point from the loser
- Give the loser a penalty card from the Penalty Stack (Wardrobe Malfunction or Extermination)

**Loser's Penalty:**
- Receive The Chop (-1 SP penalty card) added to their Backstage Archive
- If the winner also chose to give an additional penalty, the loser receives both

**Both Lose (Tie):**
- Both players receive The Chop
- No Style Points are stolen
- No additional penalties beyond The Chop

### Step 6: Archive Battle Cards
Both players place their three battle cards into their respective Backstage Archives. These cards cycle back into their decks naturally through gameplay.

---

### Battle Strategy Notes

**High LS Point Cards:** Typically more expensive cards have higher LS values (3-5), making them better in combat but costly to acquire.

**Neutral/Starter Cards:** Usually have LS: 0, making them weak in battles but cheap to play with.

**Risk Assessment:** Battling is high risk/high reward. You can gain SP quickly but also lose SP and gain junk cards if you lose.

**Untouchable Status:** Achieving Perfect Illusion grants immunity from battles for that turn, allowing you to focus purely on Stage slaying without combat risk.

## 8. STAGE RULES: THE SLAY & INSTANT SLAY

### The Standard Slay

During Phase 4, you may attempt to Slay the Active Stage if you meet its Appeal requirement.

**Requirements:**
- Your total Appeal (★) meets or exceeds the number shown on the Stage card
- You meet any prerequisite conditions listed (e.g., "Must have Blue item equipped")

**Success:**
- Take the Stage card and add it to your score pile
- Gain the Style Points (SP) listed on the card
- Immediately trigger the Grand Finale (Round End) for all players

**Failure:**
- If you do not meet the requirements, you cannot Slay
- Your turn proceeds normally to Phase 5

---

### The Instant Slay

An Instant Slay occurs when your Appeal (★) meets or exceeds the Active Stage's requirement during Phase 1 or Phase 2, before you reach Phase 4.

**Trigger Conditions:**
- During Phase 1 (Transformation) after equipping cards
- During Phase 2 (The Reveal) after applying multipliers

**Declaration:**
- You must declare the Instant Slay immediately when the condition is met
- You cannot choose to delay or ignore an Instant Slay opportunity

**Effects:**
- Claim the Stage card immediately
- Gain the Style Points listed
- Skip your remaining phases (Phase 3, 4, 5 if in Phase 1; or Phase 3, 4, 5 if in Phase 2)
- Trigger the Grand Finale immediately for all players
- The round ends instantly

**Strategic Implications:**
- Instant Slays can catch opponents off-guard, ending rounds before they finish equipping their Coordinates
- High-risk/high-reward: You forfeit your Shopping phase, losing the opportunity to improve your deck
- Consider whether the SP gain outweighs skipping purchases

---

### The Grand Finale (Round End)

When any player Slays a Stage (standard or instant), the Grand Finale begins immediately. This affects all players, not just the player who Slayed.

**Grand Finale Steps:**

1. **Archive Core Slots:** Every player archives (discards) all cards currently in their five core slots (Face, Wig, Torso, Hips, Shoes) to their Backstage Archive.

2. **Archive Accessories:** Every player archives any cards in their Accessory slot.

3. **Archive Active Stage:** The just-Slayed Stage card goes to the Stage winner's score pile (not archived—it counts for SP at game end).

4. **New Stage Reveal:** Flip the next Stage card from the Stage Deck face-up to become the new Active Stage.

5. **Resume Turn Order:** The player whose turn was interrupted (if any) completes their Phase 5 normally. Then play continues clockwise.

**Important Notes:**
- Hands are NOT archived during Grand Finale—only mat cards
- Players keep their current hands and draw normally during their next Phase 5
- All players start the new round with empty mats, building fresh Coordinates
- Any Deep Storage cards on mats are archived along with Masters during Grand Finale

## 9. WINNING THE GAME

### Victory Condition

The game ends immediately when any player reaches the Style Point (SP) target. The target varies by player count:

- **2 Players:** 20 SP
- **3 Players:** 18 SP
- **4 Players:** 16 SP
- **5 Players:** 14 SP

### Calculating Final Score

Your final SP total is calculated as follows:

**Add Style Points from:**
- Stage cards in your score pile (the SP value listed on each card)
- Any Queen abilities that grant SP
- Tokens earned during gameplay

**Subtract Penalty Points from:**
- Wardrobe Malfunction cards in your deck/discard (-1 SP each)
- The Chop cards in your deck/discard (-1 SP each)
- Extermination cards in your deck/discard (-2 SP each)

**Final Total:** The player with the highest net SP wins.

### Tiebreaker

If multiple players reach the target SP simultaneously or end with the same final score, use these tiebreakers in order:

1. **Most Stages Slayed:** Player with the most Stage cards in their score pile wins
2. **Fewest Penalty Cards:** Player with the fewest total penalty cards in deck/discard wins
3. **Most Expensive Collection:** Add up the costs (in Tips) of all cards in your deck. Highest total wins

## 10. CHARACTER ROSTER

Haute & Hazard features eleven iconic Queens, each with a unique passive or active ability that shapes their strategic approach. Choose your Queen at the start of setup and embrace their playstyle.

---

### 1. LADY LUX (Yellow Tenet)
**Archetype:** Trash Queen  
**Signature Brand:** Trash Can

**Ability:**  
Passive: When you complete a Look with 3 or more Trashed cards, gain +2 SP and draw 1 card.

**Strategy:** Build engine decks that cycle through cards quickly by trashing aggressively. Each trash brings you closer to bonus SP.

---

### 2. KAWAII-BOT (Pink Tenet)
**Archetype:** Speed Queen  
**Signature Brand:** Sugar Rush

**Ability:**  
Passive: When you reveal a Matching Look, gain +1 SP and +1 Action (play an additional card this phase).

**Strategy:** Focus on mono-color Pink builds for consistent Matching Look bonuses. The extra action allows explosive turns.

---

### 3. MOTHER MORTIS (Blue Tenet)
**Archetype:** Graveyard Queen  
**Signature Brand:** Necropolis

**Ability:**  
Active: Once per turn, play a card from your Backstage Archive as if it were in your hand.

**Strategy:** Utilize your discard pile as a second hand. Trash powerful cards intentionally, then play them directly from the Archive.

---

### 4. GREED (Gold Tenet)
**Archetype:** Resource Queen  
**Signature Brand:** Gilded Cage

**Ability:**  
Passive: Whenever you gain Tips, gain +1 extra Tip.

**Strategy:** Generate massive Tip economy. Every Tip-generating card becomes more valuable. Buy expensive cards earlier and more frequently.

---

### 5. VELVET VENOM (Blue Tenet)
**Archetype:** Sabotage Queen  
**Signature Brand:** Necropolis

**Ability:**  
Passive: When you play a Necropolis top card (Master in Torso slot), opponents discard 1 card from hand.

**Strategy:** Control opponents' resources by forcing discards. Build Blue-heavy decks with Necropolis Torso items.

---

### 6. MALDITA MANILA (Multi-Color)
**Archetype:** Fusion Queen  
**Signature Brand:** None (gains bonus with any 2 colors)

## 11. GLOSSARY OF TERMS

**Accessory** — The sixth equipment slot on your Player Mat. Excluded from Coordinate and brand matching calculations. Archived during Phase 5 cleanup (individual turn end).

**Appeal (★)** — Temporary performance resource used to Slay Stages. Resets to zero after your turn or after the Grand Finale.

**Backstage Archive** — Your personal discard pile. Cards here cycle back into your deck when you shuffle.

**Brand** — The color/faction of a fashion item (Pink, Blue, Purple, Yellow, Gold, Neutral). Determines synergies and multipliers.

**Chop, The** — A penalty card (-1 SP) given to battle losers.

**Coordinate (Coord)** — The five persistent core slots (Face, Wig, Torso, Hips, Shoes) on your Player Mat. Remains equipped until Grand Finale.

**Deep Storage** — Keyword allowing cards to remain buried under Master cards. Deep Storage cards provide brand colors for Fusion checks and activate Fusion abilities but cannot contribute numerical Appeal (★) via Fusion triggers.

**Dragdagulan** — Technical name for Phase 4 battle system (Sidewalk Slay-Down).

**Fusion Look** — State achieved when your core slots contain exactly two different brand colors. Unlocks Fusion abilities on all mat cards.

**Grand Finale** — Round end event triggered by Slaying a Stage. All players archive their core slots, and a new Stage is revealed.

**Instant Slay** — Claiming a Stage before Phase 4 (during Phase 1 or 2). Ends the round immediately for all players.

**LS Points** — Battle values (0-5) printed on cards. Used to resolve Dragdagulan combat.

**Lyric Snippet** — Flavor text read aloud during battles for performance effect. No mechanical impact.

**Master Card** — The top card in an occupied slot. Determines the slot's brand color for multiplier calculations.

**Matching Brand** — Appeal multiplier (×2) achieved when all five core slots share one brand color.

**Perfect Illusion** — Appeal multiplier (×3) plus Untouchable status achieved when all five core slots match your Queen's signature brand.

**Penalty Stack** — Deck of junk cards (Wardrobe Malfunctions, The Chop, Exterminations) given as battle penalties.

**Slay** — Claiming a Stage card by meeting its Appeal requirement. Ends the current round immediately.

**Stage** — Challenge card showing Appeal requirement and SP reward. The active Stage is the current challenge.

**Style Points (SP)** — Victory points. The first player to reach the target SP wins.

**Tenet** — Synonym for Brand. The six color-coded factions (Pink, Blue, Purple, Yellow, Gold, Neutral).

**Tips ($)** — Currency resource used to purchase cards from the Wardrobe Rack. Resets to zero after your turn.

**Triple Threat Box** — Mechanic zone on cards listing Play, Matching, and Fusion abilities.

**Untouchable** — Status granting immunity from battles. Opponents cannot challenge you to Dragdagulan.

**Wardrobe Rack** — The main deck (120 cards) and its Market Row (5 face-up cards) where players shop.

## 12. QUICK REFERENCE

### TURN STRUCTURE
1. **Transformation** → Equip cards, gain Tips/Appeal
2. **The Reveal** → Apply multipliers, trigger abilities
3. **Shopping** → Spend Tips to buy cards
4. **Slay or Battle** → Claim Stage or combat
5. **Cleanup** → Archive Accessories/hand, draw 5

### MULTIPLIERS
- **Matching Brand** (all 5 same color) → ×2 Appeal
- **Perfect Illusion** (all 5 = Queen brand) → ×3 Appeal + Untouchable

### FUSION LOOK
- Exactly 2 different colors in core slots
- Master cards: Full Fusion benefits including ★
- Deep Storage cards: Text effects only (0★)

### BATTLE (DRAGDAGULAN)
1. Both draw 3 cards
2. Read Lyric Snippets
3. Compare LS Points
4. Winner: Steal 1 SP or give penalty
5. Loser: Receive The Chop

### INSTANT SLAY
- Appeal ≥ Stage requirement in Phase 1 or 2
- Claim Stage immediately
- Skip remaining phases
- Trigger Grand Finale

### VICTORY CONDITION
**Target SP:**
- 2 Players: 20 SP
- 3 Players: 18 SP
- 4 Players: 16 SP
- 5 Players: 14 SP
