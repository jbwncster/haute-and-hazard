## 1. GAME OVERVIEW

### The Elevator Pitch

In Haute & Hazard, you are not merely wearing clothes—you are building a tactical Coordinate. Start from the dressing room floor, scavenge the bins, and curate high-octane Coords to dominate the sidewalk. This is where the card-based fashion of idol anime (Aikatsu style) meets the cutthroat strategy of a deck-builder, all wrapped in the fierce, unapologetic world of drag performance culture.

### Core Loop

Players begin with identical starter decks of basic wardrobe items and gradually improve their collections by purchasing designer fashion cards from a shared marketplace called the Wardrobe Rack. Cards are equipped to a Player Mat containing five core slots plus one bonus accessory slot, creating persistent tableau builds called Coordinates (Coords) that generate resources and enable powerful synergies when colors align properly.

The primary objective involves accumulating enough Appeal (★) through your equipped Coordinate to successfully Slay Stage challenges, which award Style Points (SP). The first player to reach the target SP total wins the competition. Secondary victory paths include engaging in direct combat through the Sidewalk Slay-Down battle system, stealing SP from opponents while inflicting penalty cards that clog their decks.

### Game Flow

Each Round consists of multiple player turns cycling through five distinct phases: Transformation (equipping cards), The Reveal (calculating resources and activating abilities), Shopping (purchasing new cards), Slay or Battle (claiming stages or fighting opponents), and Cleanup (discarding accessories and drawing new cards). Rounds end when any player successfully claims a Stage card through a Slay action, triggering the Grand Finale which resets all equipped Coordinates and begins a new competitive cycle.

## 2. COMPONENTS & TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

### Card Decks

**The Wardrobe Rack (120 cards)**
The main purchasable deck containing fashion items across six brand categories with LS Points ranging from 0 to 5. Cards feature Tips costs, Appeal values, brand affiliations, slot designations, and Triple Threat ability boxes containing Play, Matching, and Fusion triggered effects.

**The Dressing Room Floor (60 cards)**
Five identical twelve-card starter decks providing neutral basic wardrobe items. Each player receives one complete starter deck containing generic fashion pieces with minimal Appeal generation and simple resource effects.

**Thrift Store Throwbacks (16 cards)**
Permanent neutral stack cards always available for purchase. These cards provide consistent +2★ Appeal with LS Points of 0, serving as reliable but unexciting filler options when the Wardrobe Rack offers nothing desirable.

**Penalty Stack (20 cards)**
Negative cards given to players as consequences for losing battles or suffering other setbacks. The stack contains three penalty tiers: Wardrobe Malfunctions (-1 SP at game end), The Chop (-1 SP at game end), and Exterminations (-2 SP at game end). These cards clog decks and reduce final scoring.

**Stage Deck (12 cards)**
Challenge cards representing performance opportunities. Each Stage specifies an Appeal cost threshold and may include prerequisite requirements such as brand restrictions or Coordinate composition requirements. Successfully claiming Stages awards Style Points based on difficulty.

### Game Tokens

- Style Point (SP) tokens for tracking victory points
- Tip ($) tokens representing purchasing currency
- LS Point reference tokens for battle resolution
- First Player marker

### Player Components

- 5 Player Mats featuring six equipment slots (five core slots plus one accessory slot)
- 11 Queen character cards with unique passive and active abilities
- Quick reference cards showing turn structure and keyword definitions

## 3. SETUP INSTRUCTIONS

### Initial Preparation

**Step 1: The Runway**
Shuffle the Stage Deck and place it face-down in the center of the play area. Flip the top card face-up to reveal the Active Stage that players will compete to claim during the first Round.

**Step 2: The Wardrobe Rack**
Shuffle the main deck of 120 fashion cards thoroughly. Deal five cards face-up in a horizontal row accessible to all players. This forms the Wardrobe Rack marketplace where players will purchase designer items throughout the game. Place the remaining deck face-down next to the row as the draw pile.

**Step 3: The Thrift Store Throwbacks**
Place all sixteen Thrift Store Throwback cards face-up in a separate stack near the Wardrobe Rack. These permanent neutral options remain available for purchase at all times as a fallback when the main marketplace offers nothing appealing.

**Step 4: The Haus**
Each player selects or randomly draws one Queen character card from the roster of eleven available Queens. Take your chosen Queen and read its passive or active ability aloud to ensure all players understand its mechanical implications. Place your Queen face-up in front of you where it remains visible throughout the game.

Each player receives one complete twelve-card Dressing Room Floor starter deck. Shuffle your starter deck thoroughly to randomize the card order, then draw five cards to form your opening hand.

**Step 5: Token Pools**
Place all Style Point tokens, Tip tokens, and LS Point reference materials in the center supply where all players can reach them easily. These communal resources will be drawn from throughout gameplay.

**Step 6: Underdog's Edge**
The player with the lowest current Style Point total takes the first turn. Since all players begin at zero SP, determine the starting player randomly through any fair method (youngest player, dice roll, etc.). Play proceeds clockwise from the starting player.

Setup is complete. The starting player begins Phase 1: Transformation.

## 4. TURN STRUCTURE

Each player's turn consists of five distinct phases executed in strict sequential order. Players cannot skip phases or return to previous phases during the same turn.

### PHASE 1: TRANSFORMATION

**Overview:** Equip fashion cards from your hand to your Player Mat slots.

**Core Slots (The Coordinate):**
Your Player Mat contains five core equipment positions that comprise your Coordinate (Coord):
- Face (makeup/cosmetics slot)
- Wig (hair/headpiece slot)
- Torso (upper body/dress slot)
- Hips (lower body/pants/skirt slot)
- Shoes (footwear slot)

**Bonus Slot:**
- Accessory (jewelry/props slot — does not count toward Coordinate brand matching)

**Equipping Rules:**
You may play any number of cards from your hand to available mat slots during this phase. Each slot can hold only one visible Master card at a time. When equipping a card to an occupied slot, you must choose whether to replace the existing Master (sending it to your Backstage Archive discard pile) or use the Deep Storage keyword if available on the new card.

**Immediate Harvest:**
As each card is equipped, immediately gain any Tips ($) and Appeal (★) listed in its stat line. These resources accumulate in your personal pools for use during subsequent phases of this turn.

**Instant Slay Check:**
If your total harvested Appeal (★) meets or exceeds the Active Stage's cost at any point during Phase 1, and any card you just equipped has the Instant Slay keyword, you may immediately claim the Stage. This triggers the Grand Finale, ending the Round for all players and skipping directly to cleanup procedures.

### PHASE 2: THE REVEAL

**Overview:** Calculate multipliers and activate conditional abilities based on your equipped Coordinate configuration.

**Step 1: Deep Storage Resolution**
Examine all cards currently equipped to your Player Mat. Any card with the Deep Storage keyword text remains buried beneath the Master card in its slot. All other covered cards that lack this keyword are immediately moved to your Backstage Archive discard pile. Deep Storage cards provide brand colors for fusion checks but cannot contribute numerical Appeal values.

**Step 2: Fusion Check**
Count the number of distinct brand colors visible across your five core slots (Face, Wig, Torso, Hips, Shoes). If you have exactly two different brand colors present, you have achieved a Fusion Look. Activate all Fusion ability triggers listed in the Triple Threat boxes of every card currently on your Player Mat.

**Fusion Appeal Distinction:**
- Master cards (top visible cards in slots) provide both their Fusion ability text effects AND any Appeal (★) bonus listed in that Fusion trigger
- Deep Storage cards (buried beneath Masters) provide their Fusion ability text effects but contribute 0★ Appeal regardless of what the trigger text states

**Step 3: Brand Matching Multipliers**
After resolving Fusion triggers, check for brand uniformity:

**Matching Brand:** If all five core slots contain Master cards sharing a single identical brand color, multiply your total Appeal by 2.

**Perfect Illusion:** If all five core slots contain Master cards matching your Queen's signature Tenet brand, multiply your total Appeal by 3 AND gain Untouchable status (immunity from Dragdagulan battles) for this Round.

**Instant Slay Check:**
If your total calculated Appeal (★) after multipliers meets or exceeds the Active Stage's cost, and you have not yet performed a Slay this Round, you may immediately claim the Stage. This triggers the Grand Finale, ending the Round for all players and skipping Phases 3 and 4.

### PHASE 3: SHOPPING

**Overview:** Spend accumulated Tips to purchase cards from available marketplaces.

**The Wardrobe Rack:**
Examine the five face-up cards in the main marketplace row. Each card displays its Tips ($) cost in the upper corner. You may purchase any number of cards you can afford by paying their listed costs. Purchased cards go directly to your Backstage Archive discard pile (not your hand). After each purchase, immediately replace the bought card by drawing from the main deck to maintain five visible options.

**The Thrift Store Throwbacks:**
You may purchase cards from the permanent neutral stack instead of (or in addition to) buying from the Wardrobe Rack. Thrift Store Throwbacks cost Tips as listed on the stack and provide reliable +2★ Appeal with LS Point value of 0.

**Optional Purchasing:**
You are never required to spend Tips during this phase. Unspent Tips do not carry over to future turns and are lost during Phase 5 cleanup.

### PHASE 4: SLAY OR BATTLE (THE CHOICE)

**Overview:** Attempt to claim the Active Stage or engage an opponent in combat.

**Option A: The Slay (Standard)**
Compare your total Appeal (★) calculated during Phase 2 to the Active Stage's cost requirement. Additionally verify that you meet any prerequisite conditions listed on the Stage card (such as brand restrictions, equipment requirements, or Coordinate composition demands).

If both the Appeal threshold and all prerequisites are satisfied, you successfully Slay the Stage. Take the Stage card and place it in your personal score pile, gaining the Style Points (SP) listed on the card. This immediately triggers the Grand Finale, ending the Round for all players.

**Option B: Dragdagulan (Sidewalk Slay-Down)**
Instead of attempting to Slay the Stage, you may challenge another player to direct combat. See Section 7: Dragdagulan for complete battle resolution rules. Combat winners steal Style Points or inflict penalty cards on losers.

**Option C: Pass**
You may choose to do neither Slay nor Battle, preserving your equipped Coordinate for future turns while other players take their actions.

### PHASE 5: CLEANUP (INDIVIDUAL TURN END)

**Overview:** Reset temporary resources and draw new cards for your next turn.

**Step 1: Archive Hand**
Discard all cards currently in your hand to your Backstage Archive discard pile. Your hand size resets to zero at the end of each turn.

**Step 2: Archive Accessories**
Any cards equipped to your Accessory slot are automatically discarded to your Backstage Archive. The Accessory slot provides temporary benefits for one turn only and does not persist across turns.

**Step 3: Persistent Core Slots**
Cards equipped to your five core slots (Face, Wig, Torso, Hips, Shoes) remain face-up on your Player Mat. These cards persist across multiple turns, continuing to generate resources and enable synergies until you choose to replace them or a Grand Finale Round reset occurs.

**Step 4: Draw New Hand**
Draw five cards from your deck to form your new hand for your next turn. If your deck is depleted, shuffle your Backstage Archive discard pile to create a new draw deck and continue drawing until you have five cards.

**Step 5: Pass Turn**
Your turn ends. The next player clockwise begins their Phase 1: Transformation.

## 5. STACKING LOGIC: MASTER VS. DEEP STORAGE

### The Master Card

The Master card is the top visible card in any equipment slot. Only the Master card's brand color counts for Matching Brand and Perfect Illusion multiplier calculations. The Master card fully contributes all its printed stats (Tips, Appeal) and can provide both utility effects and numerical bonuses when Fusion triggers activate.

### Deep Storage Keyword

Cards with the Deep Storage keyword text may be played beneath an existing Master card rather than replacing it. Deep Storage cards remain buried under the Master card and are not discarded during Phase 2 cleanup processes.

**Fusion Trigger Contributions:**
Deep Storage cards contribute their brand color toward Fusion Look requirements (the two-color check). When a Fusion Look is active, Deep Storage cards activate their Fusion ability text from the Triple Threat box. However, Deep Storage cards cannot contribute numerical Appeal (★) values through Fusion triggers—they provide utility effects only.

**Strategic Implications:**
Deep Storage allows players to build multi-layered equipment slots where hidden cards provide brand color diversity for Fusion triggers while the visible Master card displays a different brand for potential Matching multipliers. This creates complex decision trees around single-brand focus versus multi-brand flexibility.

### Stacking Example

You have a Blue brand Wig as your Master card. You draw a Pink brand Wig with Deep Storage keyword. You play the Pink Wig into Deep Storage beneath your Blue Master. Your Wig slot now shows Blue (Master) but contains Pink (Deep Storage). If you later achieve a Fusion Look with two colors present across your core slots, the Pink Deep Storage Wig will activate its Fusion ability text but cannot contribute any ★ Appeal listed in that Fusion trigger.

## 6. THE THRIFT STORE THROWBACKS

The Thrift Store Throwbacks represent a permanent marketplace option available throughout the entire game. This sixteen-card neutral stack remains face-up and accessible, offering consistent fallback purchases when the main Wardrobe Rack contains nothing desirable or affordable.

**Stat Profile:**
- Appeal: +2★ (fixed value)
- LS Points: 0 (for Dragdagulan combat resolution)
- Brand: Neutral (does not contribute to Matching Brand or Fusion Looks)
- Cost: Variable Tips depending on card type

**Strategic Role:**
Thrift Store Throwbacks serve as deck-building filler that improves upon starter deck cards without requiring lucky draws from the Wardrobe Rack. They provide reliable Appeal generation for Stage Slays while maintaining low LS Point values that make them poor combat options. Experienced players treat Throwbacks as temporary stepping stones toward acquiring premium designer brands.

## 7. DRAGDAGULAN: THE SIDEWALK SLAY-DOWN

Dragdagulan (the Sidewalk Slay-Down) represents direct player-versus-player combat. When you choose the Battle option during Phase 4, you initiate a Dragdagulan against a target opponent following this resolution sequence.

### The Draw

Both the aggressor (attacking player) and defender each draw three cards from their respective decks. These three cards are drawn specifically for the battle and are not added to players' normal hands.

### The Lyric Rule

Players read the Lyric Snippet flavor text from each of their three drawn cards aloud in the order drawn. This creates a six-line collaborative performance combining both players' card texts. The Lyric Rule emphasizes the game's drag performance thematic while providing brief entertainment during combat resolution.

### LS Point Calculation

After reading Lyric Snippets, players reveal all three cards simultaneously. Sum the LS Point values (ranging from 0 to 5) printed on each card. The player with the higher total LS Points wins the Dragdagulan.

**Ties:** If both players have identical LS Point totals, the battle results in a draw with no winner. Both players still suffer the consequences (see Outcome below) as if both had lost.

### Outcome

**Winner:** The victorious player chooses one of two rewards:
- Steal 1 Style Point from the defeated opponent
- Give the defeated opponent one Penalty card from the Penalty Stack (Wardrobe Malfunction, The Chop, or Extermination)

**Loser:** The defeated player receives one Chop penalty card (-1 SP) added to their Backstage Archive discard pile. This card will cycle into their deck and reduce final scoring.

**Realness Keyword Protection:** If the defeated player's Master card in any core slot has the Realness keyword, they do not receive The Chop penalty. This keyword provides immunity to the standard loser penalty while still allowing the winner to claim their chosen reward.

### Post-Battle

All six cards drawn for the Dragdagulan (three per player) are moved to their respective owners' Backstage Archive discard piles. The battle does not end the Round—play continues with the next player's turn proceeding clockwise.

## 8. STAGE RULES: THE SLAY & INSTANT SLAY

### The Slay (Standard)

The standard Slay occurs during Phase 4 when you declare your intention to claim the Active Stage. You must meet two requirements: your total Appeal (★) calculated during Phase 2 must equal or exceed the Stage's cost requirement, and you must satisfy all prerequisite conditions listed on the Stage card (brand requirements, equipment restrictions, Coordinate composition demands, etc.).

Upon successful Slay, take the Stage card and place it in your personal score pile. Gain the Style Points (SP) printed on the Stage card. This immediately triggers the Grand Finale Round-ending sequence.

### The Instant Slay

An Instant Slay occurs when your Appeal (★) meets or exceeds the Active Stage's cost during Phase 1 (Transformation) or Phase 2 (The Reveal) AND one of your equipped cards contains the Instant Slay keyword instruction.

**Timing:** The Instant Slay may be declared the moment your Appeal total reaches the threshold, even mid-phase as cards are being equipped.

**Effect:** Claim the Active Stage immediately, gaining its Style Points. Skip Phase 3 (Shopping) and Phase 4 (Slay or Battle) entirely for all players.

**Impact:** The Round ends instantly for all players. The Grand Finale triggers, resetting all equipped Coordinates.

**Strategic Implications:** Instant Slay creates tension around turn order and equipment timing. Later players in turn order risk losing opportunities if earlier players Instant Slay before they can optimize their Coordinates or spend accumulated Tips.

### The Grand Finale (Round End)

When any player successfully Slays a Stage (standard or Instant), the Grand Finale immediately triggers. All players follow this sequence:

1. **Archive Coordinates:** Every player moves all cards from their five core slots (Face, Wig, Torso, Hips, Shoes) to their Backstage Archive discard piles. Accessories have already been discarded during individual Phase 5 cleanups.

2. **Archive Stage:** The claimed Stage card is removed from the active play area and placed in the winner's personal score pile.

3. **Reveal New Stage:** Flip the top card of the Stage Deck face-up to become the new Active Stage for the next Round.

4. **Round Counter Advances:** If using variant rules or tracking Round numbers for other purposes, increment the Round counter.

5. **Continue Play:** The next player in clockwise turn order begins Phase 1 of their turn, starting a new Round with reset Coordinates.

## 10. PENALTIES & WARDROBE MALFUNCTIONS

The Penalty Stack contains negative consequence cards that players accumulate through losing Dragdagulan battles or suffering other setbacks. These cards serve as deck-building pollution that reduces strategic efficiency and subtracts Style Points at game end.

### Penalty Types

**Wardrobe Malfunction (-1 SP)**
Minor fashion disaster. When this card is drawn during normal gameplay, it provides no beneficial effects and contributes zero LS Points in battles. At game end, subtract 1 Style Point from your final score for each Wardrobe Malfunction in your deck or discard pile.

**The Chop (-1 SP)**
Standard battle loss penalty. Identical mechanical profile to Wardrobe Malfunction. Functions as deck clog that reduces efficiency. At game end, subtract 1 Style Point per Chop card.

**Extermination (-2 SP)**
Severe penalty for catastrophic failures. This card represents complete elimination from a category or massive performance disaster. At game end, subtract 2 Style Points per Extermination card in your deck or discard pile.

### Penalty Strategy

Penalty cards cannot be voluntarily removed from decks through normal gameplay mechanics. They cycle through your deck repeatedly, appearing in hands as dead draws that provide no resources. The accumulation of penalties creates a death spiral where combat losses lead to deck pollution which leads to weaker future combat performance which leads to more losses.

Players must either avoid Dragdagulan entirely (focusing purely on Stage Slays) or build specialized combat decks with high LS Point concentrations that minimize penalty accumulation risk.

## 11. WINNING THE GAME

### Victory Condition

The game ends immediately when any player reaches the target Style Point (SP) threshold. The standard victory condition requires 30 Style Points, though players may agree to adjust this target for shorter or longer games.

### Scoring Sources

**Stage Slays:** The primary SP source. Each Stage card awards Style Points when successfully claimed. Stage values range from 4 SP (easy challenges) to 10 SP (difficult challenges).

**Character Abilities:** Some Queens provide passive or active abilities that award bonus Style Points for meeting specific conditions (completing Matching Looks, trashing cards, etc.).

**Combat Victories:** Winning Dragdagulan battles allows stealing 1 SP from defeated opponents, creating relative SP gain through opponent loss.

### End-Game Scoring

When the game ends via reaching the SP threshold, additional final scoring occurs:

1. **Active SP Total:** Your current Style Point accumulation from Stages and abilities

2. **Penalty Subtraction:** Count all Wardrobe Malfunctions, Chops, and Exterminations in your deck and discard pile. Subtract the listed SP values from your total.

3. **Final Score:** Active SP minus Penalty SP equals your final score.

The player with the highest final score wins. In the event of a tie, the tied player with fewer total cards in their deck wins (rewarding efficient deck-building). If still tied, the tied player with the most expensive card collection (sum of all Tips costs) wins.

## 13. GLOSSARY OF TERMS

**Accessory** — The sixth equipment slot on your Player Mat. Cards equipped here do not count toward Coordinate brand calculations and are discarded during Phase 5 cleanup after each individual turn.

**Appeal (★)** — Temporary resource generated by equipped cards and used to meet Stage Slay requirements. Appeal resets to zero at the end of each individual turn or Round.

**Backstage Archive** — Your personal discard pile where used cards, discarded cards, and archived equipment go after leaving play. When your deck is depleted, shuffle the Backstage Archive to create a new draw deck.

**Brand** — The color/faction affiliation of a fashion card (Pink, Blue, Purple, Yellow, Red, Green, or Neutral). Brands determine eligibility for Matching Brand multipliers and Fusion Look triggers.

**Chop, The** — A penalty card worth -1 SP at game end. Given to players who lose Dragdagulan battles (unless protected by Realness keyword).

**Coordinate (Coord)** — The five persistent core slots on your Player Mat: Face, Wig, Torso, Hips, and Shoes. Cards in these slots remain equipped across multiple turns until replaced or a Grand Finale occurs.

**Deep Storage** — Keyword allowing cards to be played beneath existing Master cards rather than replacing them. Deep Storage cards contribute brand colors for Fusion checks and activate Fusion abilities, but cannot contribute numerical Appeal (★) values.

**Dragdagulan** — The technical name for Phase 4 combat. Also called Sidewalk Slay-Down. Players draw three cards, read Lyric Snippets, compare LS Point totals, and resolve winner/loser consequences.

**Fusion Look** — Achieved when your five core slots contain exactly two different brand colors. Triggers all Fusion abilities in Triple Threat boxes on equipped cards. Master cards provide utility and Appeal through Fusion; Deep Storage cards provide utility only (0★).

**Grand Finale** — Round-ending event triggered when any player successfully Slays a Stage. All players archive their equipped Coordinates, a new Stage is revealed, and a new Round begins.

**Instant Slay** — Special Slay that occurs during Phase 1 or Phase 2 if Appeal meets Stage cost and an equipped card has Instant Slay keyword. Immediately ends the Round for all players, skipping Phases 3 and 4.

**LS Points** — Lyric Snippet Points, numerical values (0-5) printed on cards and used to resolve Dragdagulan combat. Higher total LS Points wins the battle.

**Lyric Snippet** — Flavor text on cards representing song lyrics or performance quotes. Read aloud during Dragdagulan battles in the order cards are drawn.

**Matching Brand** — Achieved when all five core slots contain Master cards sharing one identical brand color. Multiplies total Appeal by 2.

**Master Card** — The top visible card in any equipment slot. Only the Master's brand counts for Matching Brand and Perfect Illusion. Masters can contribute Appeal through Fusion triggers.

**Perfect Illusion** — Achieved when all five core slots contain Master cards matching your Queen's signature Tenet brand. Multiplies total Appeal by 3 and grants Untouchable status (immunity from Dragdagulan).

**Realness** — Keyword providing immunity to The Chop penalty when losing Dragdagulan battles. Player still suffers opponent's chosen reward (SP steal or different penalty card) but avoids the automatic Chop.

**Slay** — Successfully claiming an Active Stage card by meeting its Appeal cost and prerequisites during Phase 4. Triggers the Grand Finale, ending the current Round.

**Stage** — Challenge card representing performance opportunities. Shows Appeal cost, prerequisites, and SP reward. Revealed from Stage Deck and remains active until claimed via Slay.

**Style Points (SP)** — Victory points. Gained from Slaying Stages, character abilities, and combat victories. Game ends when any player reaches target SP threshold (standard: 30 SP).

**Tips ($)** — Currency used to purchase cards from the Wardrobe Rack or Thrift Store Throwbacks during Phase 3. Tips are generated by equipped cards and Action cards, and reset to zero at end of turn.

**Triple Threat Box** — Mechanical zone on fashion cards containing three triggered abilities: Play (immediate when equipped), Matching (active with Matching Brand), and Fusion (active with Fusion Look).

**Untouchable** — Status granting immunity from Dragdagulan battles. Opponents cannot target you for combat while you have Untouchable status. Gained through Perfect Illusion or certain card abilities.

**Wardrobe Rack** — The main marketplace. Five face-up cards from the 120-card deck, refreshed after each purchase. Primary source of designer fashion cards for deck improvement.

## 14. QUICK REFERENCE

### Turn Structure
1. **Transformation** — Equip cards to Mat slots
2. **The Reveal** — Calculate multipliers, activate abilities
3. **Shopping** — Buy cards with Tips ($)
4. **Slay or Battle** — Claim Stage or fight opponent
5. **Cleanup** — Archive hand and Accessories, draw 5 cards

### Core Slots (The Coordinate)
- Face, Wig, Torso, Hips, Shoes
- Persist across turns until Grand Finale
- Only Masters count for brand matching
