## 1. GAME OVERVIEW

**Players:** 2-5  
**Time:** 45-60 minutes  
**Age:** 14+

Haute & Hazard is a competitive deck-building card game where players take on the role of drag queens competing in fashion challenges. Build your look by acquiring fashion items from the Boutique, equip them to your Player Mat, and accumulate enough Appeal (★) to Slay the Stage and earn Style Points (SP). The queen with the most SP at the end wins!

### Core Concepts

- **Deck-Building**: Purchase cards from the shared Boutique to improve your deck
- **Tableau Management**: Equip fashion items to 5 core slots on your Player Mat
- **Resource Management**: Balance Tips ($) for purchasing and Appeal (★) for winning
- **Brand Synergies**: Match colors and brands for powerful combo effects

## 3. SETUP

### Initial Setup

1. **Choose Your Queen**: Each player selects or randomly draws 1 Queen card. Place it face-up in front of you.

2. **Distribute Starter Decks**: Each player receives:
   - 7 Basic Beat
   - 3 Messy Lip Sync
   - 2 Chapstick
   
   Shuffle these 12 cards to form your starting deck. Draw 5 cards as your starting hand.

3. **Prepare the Boutique**:
   - Shuffle the Main Deck (all fashion items, Vogue cards, etc.)
   - Deal 5 cards face-up in a row - this is the Market Row
   - Place the remaining deck face-down as the draw pile

4. **Prepare the Stage**:
   - Shuffle the Stage Deck
   - Place it face-down near the center
   - Flip the top card face-up - this is the Active Stage

5. **Prepare the Extermination Deck**:
   - Shuffle all Extermination cards
   - Place face-down as a separate deck

6. **Token Pool**: Place all SP, Tip, and Defense tokens in a central supply.

7. **Determine First Player**: Randomly select who goes first.

## 4. TURN STRUCTURE

Each turn consists of 5 phases, executed in order:

### PHASE 1: Transformation (Equip)

**Equip fashion items from your hand to your Player Mat.**

Your Player Mat has 5 core equipment slots:
- 💋 **Face** (Makeup slot)
- 💇 **Wig** (Hair slot)  
- 👗 **Body** (Torso/Dress slot)
- 👠 **Shoes** (Footwear slot)
- 💍 **Accessory** (Bonus slot)

**Rules:**
- You may equip any number of cards from your hand this phase
- Each slot can hold only 1 card
- Replacing a card: The old card goes to your discard pile
- **Accessories are temporary**: They are discarded at the end of Phase 5
- Cards remain equipped across turns until replaced

### PHASE 2: The Reveal (Generate Resources)

**Calculate your Appeal (★) and Tips ($) for this turn.**

1. **Trigger Card Abilities**: Activate any "On Reveal" or "Set" abilities on equipped cards

2. **Calculate Appeal**:
   - Add up all ★ values on equipped fashion items
   - Add ★ from Action cards you play this turn
   - Apply any Set bonuses (see Brand Matching below)

3. **Calculate Tips**:
   - Your base Tip pool starts at 0 each turn
   - Add $ from Action cards you play
   - Add $ from card abilities

**Brand Matching:**

- **Matching Look**: All 5 core slots share the SAME brand color → multiply total Appeal × 2
- **Fusion Look**: Exactly 2 different brand colors across core slots → activate special "Set" abilities
- Accessories don't count toward brand matching

### PHASE 3: The Shopping Spree (Buy Cards)

**Spend Tips ($) to purchase cards from the Market Row.**

- Each card shows its cost in the top-left corner
- Pay the cost and add the card to your discard pile (not your hand)
- You may buy multiple cards if you have enough Tips
- After each purchase, immediately replace the card from the draw deck
- You may choose NOT to buy anything

**Trash Pile:**
- If a card effect says "Trash", it goes to a separate Trash pile (not your discard)
- Trashed cards are removed from your deck unless retrieved by specific abilities
- Some cards allow you to buy from the Trash pile

### PHASE 4: Slay or Battle

**Attempt to claim the Active Stage or engage in combat.**

**Option A: Slay the Stage**
- Compare your total Appeal (★) to the Stage's target number
- Check if you meet any prerequisite requirements listed on the Stage
- **If successful**: Take the Stage card and add it to your score pile. Gain the listed SP. Flip a new Stage immediately.
- **If failed**: Nothing happens - end your turn

**Option B: Battle (Optional)**
- If you DON'T want to Slay, you may challenge another player to a Battle
- See Advanced Rules: Combat for detailed Battle rules
- Battles award SP for the winner and penalties for the loser

**Option C: Pass**
- You may choose to do neither

### PHASE 5: Cleanup (End Turn)

1. **Discard Accessories**: Any Accessory cards equipped are discarded
2. **Discard Action Cards**: Any Action cards played this turn go to discard
3. **Draw**: Draw up to 5 cards (draw cards until you have 5 in hand)
4. **Pass Turn**: The next player begins their Phase 1

**Important:** Fashion items in core slots (Face, Wig, Body, Shoes) stay equipped!

## 5. CARD TYPES & ABILITIES

### Fashion Items (Equipment Cards)

Fashion items have:
- **Slot**: Which mat slot they occupy (Wig, Body, Shoes, Face, Accessory)
- **Cost**: Tips ($) required to purchase
- **Stats**: Appeal (★) value provided
- **Brand**: Color/faction (Pink, Blue, Purple, Yellow)
- **Abilities**: Special effects (see below)

### Action Cards

Action cards are played from your hand during Phase 2 or 3:
- Provide immediate Tips ($) or Appeal (★)
- Have one-time-use effects
- Go to discard pile after use
- Examples: Basic Beat, Messy Lip Sync, Vogue cards

### Ability Types

**Triple Threat Structure** (bottom of fashion cards):

1. **PLAY** (Immediate): Triggers when first equipped
2. **SET** (Conditional): Triggers if specific conditions are met
3. **FLAIR** (Passive): Always active while equipped

Common Ability Keywords:

- **Draw X**: Draw X cards from your deck
- **Synergy**: Bonus if you meet a condition (e.g., "If you have a Pink Wig...")
- **Defense**: Provides protection in Battles (see Combat rules)
- **Attack**: Harms opponents in Battles
- **Wild**: Counts as any color for Set matching purposes
- **Trash**: Remove a card from the game (to Trash pile)
- **Chain**: Part of a Vogue combo sequence (see Vogue Rules)

## 6. WINNING THE GAME

### Game End Trigger

The game ends immediately when **ANY** of these conditions are met:

1. **The Stage Deck is depleted** (all 12 Stages have been Slayed)
2. **A player reaches 30 SP**
3. **The Boutique deck runs out** (Market Row cannot be refilled)

### Final Scoring

1. **Stage Points**: Add up SP from all Stage cards you've Slayed
2. **Collection Points**: Some cards have "Worth X SP at end of game" (e.g., Sequined Gown)
3. **Penalty Points**: Subtract SP for any Extermination cards in your deck/discard
4. **Total**: The player with the most SP wins!

**Tiebreaker**: If tied, the player with the most expensive card collection (sum of all card costs) wins.

## 7. ADVANCED RULES

### Combat System (Battles)

When you declare a Battle:

1. **Choose Target**: Select an opponent
2. **Calculate Attack/Defense**:
   - Your Attack = Appeal (★) + Attack bonuses from cards
   - Your Defense = Base 0 + Defense bonuses from cards
   - Opponent calculates the same

3. **Resolve Battle**:
   - Compare: (Your Attack - Opponent's Defense) vs (Their Attack - Your Defense)
   - Higher value wins
   - Ties = no winner (both take penalties)

4. **Consequences**:
   - **Winner**: Gains 2 SP + any Bloodlust/Battle bonuses from their Queen
   - **Loser**: Receives 1 Extermination card (added to discard pile)

### Vogue Combo System

Vogue cards create powerful chains:

1. **Hands Starter**: "Ten, Ten, Tens!" - Starts the combo
2. **Walk Link**: "Catwalk Strut" - Must be played immediately after Hands
3. **Finish**: "Spin & Dip" - Must be played after Walk

**Chain Bonus**: If you complete all 3, you get massive Appeal and draw bonuses!

### Brand Synergies

Each brand has unique mechanics:

- **💗 Pink (Pop)**: Card draw and resource generation
- **♠️ Blue (Horror)**: Attack and opponent disruption  
- **💎 Purple (Glamour)**: Defense and Style Point bonuses
- **⭐ Yellow (Camp)**: Trash mechanics and wild colors

### Special Abilities

**Once Per Turn** abilities (like Rat Tail Purse v3.1):
- Can only be activated ONCE during your entire turn
- Track with a token if needed

**Passive** abilities:
- Always active as long as the card is equipped
- Examples: "Pink cards cost -1 Tip" (Holographic Bodysuit)

**Immune** effects:
- Completely prevent specific effects (e.g., "Immune to Discard" on Pageant Updo)

## 8. GLOSSARY

**Appeal (★)**: The primary resource for Slaying Stages. Generated by fashion items and actions.

**Accessory**: The 5th equipment slot. Unlike core slots, Accessories are discarded at end of turn.

**Battle**: Combat between players. Winner gains SP, loser receives Extermination penalty.

**Brand**: The color/faction of a fashion item (Pink, Blue, Purple, Yellow).

**Boutique**: The main deck of purchasable cards.

**Core Slots**: The 4 permanent equipment slots (Face, Wig, Body, Shoes) that persist across turns.

**Extermination**: Penalty cards that clog your deck and reduce SP.

**Fusion Look**: Having exactly 2 different brand colors equipped in your core slots.

**Market Row**: The 5 face-up cards available for purchase from the Boutique.

**Matching Look**: Having all 5 core slots be the same brand color (grants Appeal × 2).

**Player Mat**: Your personal board with 5 equipment slots.

**Slay**: Successfully claiming a Stage card by meeting its requirements.

**Stage**: Challenge cards that award Style Points when Slayed.

**Style Points (SP)**: Victory points. Game ends at 30 SP or when Stages run out.

**Tips ($)**: The currency used to buy cards from the Market Row.

**Trash**: Permanently removing a card from your deck (different from discard).

**Wild**: A card that counts as any color for Set/Synergy purposes.

## 9. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**Q: Can I replace an equipped card with another card of the same slot type?**  
A: Yes! The old card goes to your discard pile. You can do this during Phase 1.

**Q: Do Accessories count toward Matching/Fusion Looks?**  
A: No. Only the 4 core slots (Face, Wig, Body, Shoes) count for brand matching.

**Q: What happens if I can't draw 5 cards during Phase 5?**  
A: Draw as many as you can. If your deck is empty, shuffle your discard pile to create a new deck.

**Q: Can I use Rat Tail Purse multiple times in one turn?**  
A: No. As of v3.1, Rat Tail Purse explicitly says "ONCE PER TURN" to prevent infinite loops.

**Q: What's the maximum Defense I can get from Feather Boa?**  
A: +3 Defense (as of v3.1). Even if you have 5 Purple cards equipped, the cap is +3.

**Q: Do I HAVE to Slay if I meet the Stage requirements?**  
A: No. Slaying is optional. You can choose to Battle or Pass instead.

**Q: Can I look at my discard pile?**  
A: Yes, at any time. Both your discard pile and the Trash pile are public information.

**Q: What happens when the Stage deck runs out?**  
A: The game ends immediately. Proceed to final scoring.

**Q: Can I buy the same card multiple times in one turn?**  
A: Yes, as long as you have enough Tips and it's available in the Market Row or Trash pile (if allowed).

**Q: Do Queen passive abilities trigger automatically?**  
A: Yes. Queen abilities are always active unless they specify "Once per turn" or "Once per game".
