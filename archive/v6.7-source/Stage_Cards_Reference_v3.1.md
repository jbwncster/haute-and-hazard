## 📋 STAGE DECK OVERVIEW

**Total Cards**: 12 unique Stage challenges  
**Card Size**: Poker-size (2.5" × 3.5")  
**Back Design**: Spotlight + "STAGE" text  
**Shuffle**: Before each game

Each Stage card represents a themed performance challenge. Players must meet the **Win Condition** (prerequisite) AND accumulate enough **Appeal (★)** to claim the Stage and earn **Style Points (SP)**.

## 🌟 COMPLETE STAGE LISTING

### 1. THE MONSTER BALL
**Theme**: Horror performance  
**Win Condition**: Must have Horror (Blue) item equipped  
**Target Appeal**: 10★  
**SP Reward**: 5 SP  
**Flavor**: *"Scare us stiff."*

**Strategy**: Equip at least one Blue (Necropolis, Slasher, or Void) item, then accumulate 10★ total Appeal. Blue Tenet-focused builds excel here.

---

### 2. ANIME MATSURI  
**Theme**: Pop culture celebration  
**Win Condition**: Must have Pop (Pink) item equipped  
**Target Appeal**: 12★  
**SP Reward**: 6 SP  
**Flavor**: *"Kawaii desu ne!"*

**Strategy**: Equip a Pink (Sugar Rush or Hyper-Glitch) item and build Appeal. Slightly harder than Monster Ball but worth more SP.

---

### 3. THE FILTH FLOOR
**Theme**: Trashy elegance  
**Win Condition**: Must Trash 1 card from hand  
**Target Appeal**: 10★  
**SP Reward**: 5 SP  
**Flavor**: *"Get dirty."*

**Strategy**: Requires you to sacrifice a card from hand to the Trash pile during your turn. Yellow Tenet queens (Trashique, Madam Voodoo) love this Stage.

---

### 4. GRAND FINALE
**Theme**: Ultimate elegance  
**Win Condition**: Must have a Matching Look (Full Set)  
**Target Appeal**: 20★  
**SP Reward**: 10 SP  
**Flavor**: *"Category is: Eleganza Extravaganza."*

**Strategy**: The most valuable Stage! Requires all 4 core slots to share the same brand color. With Matching Look's × 2 multiplier, you only need base 10★ equipment.

---

### 5. DUALITY BALL
**Theme**: Contrasting aesthetics  
**Win Condition**: Must have a Fusion Look (Mixed Set)  
**Target Appeal**: 15★  
**SP Reward**: 8 SP  
**Flavor**: *"Show us two sides of the same coin."*

**Strategy**: Requires exactly 2 different brand colors in your core slots. Fusion builds that trigger "Set" abilities shine here. Moderate difficulty, good reward.

---

### 6. FACE FACE FACE
**Theme**: Makeup artistry  
**Win Condition**: Must have a Face card equipped  
**Target Appeal**: 8★  
**SP Reward**: 4 SP  
**Flavor**: *"No butterfaces allowed."*

**Strategy**: Easiest Stage to Slay! Just equip any Face slot card and accumulate 8★. Great for early game.

---

### 7. WITCHING HOUR
**Theme**: Occult performance  
**Win Condition**: Must have a Swamp Witch or Void item  
**Target Appeal**: 13★  
**SP Reward**: 7 SP  
**Flavor**: *"Cast a spell on the judges."*

**Strategy**: Requires specific rare brands (Swamp Witch or Void). These are Yellow and Blue Ultra Rare brands. High difficulty, solid reward.

---

### 8. DUNGEON DRAG
**Theme**: Dark glamour  
**Win Condition**: Must have Slasher or Velvet Trap item  
**Target Appeal**: 14★  
**SP Reward**: 7 SP  
**Flavor**: *"Leather and lace."*

**Strategy**: Needs Blue Uncommon (Slasher) or Purple Common (Velvet Trap). Moderate to high difficulty, good SP payout.

---

### 9. CIRCUS BERSERKUS
**Theme**: Chaotic camp  
**Win Condition**: Must have Big Top or Sugar Rush item  
**Target Appeal**: 11★  
**SP Reward**: 6 SP  
**Flavor**: *"Send in the clowns."*

**Strategy**: Requires Yellow Uncommon (Big Top) or Pink Common (Sugar Rush). Accessible brands, moderate difficulty.

---

### 10. NEON NIGHTS
**Theme**: Cyberpunk rave  
**Win Condition**: Must have Hyper-Glitch item  
**Target Appeal**: 12★  
**SP Reward**: 6 SP  
**Flavor**: *"Glow in the dark."*

**Strategy**: Needs Pink Uncommon (Hyper-Glitch). Similar difficulty to Anime Matsuri but more specific brand requirement.

---

### 11. ROYAL RUMBLE
**Theme**: Pageantry  
**Win Condition**: Must have Gilded Cage item  
**Target Appeal**: 16★  
**SP Reward**: 8 SP  
**Flavor**: *"Heavy is the head."*

**Strategy**: Requires Purple Ultra Rare (Gilded Cage). Very difficult to meet prerequisites, but excellent SP reward. Bella Donna and Lady Lux queens excel.

---

### 12. APOCALYPSE NOW
**Theme**: End times fashion  
**Win Condition**: Must have Trash Can or Necropolis item  
**Target Appeal**: 14★  
**SP Reward**: 7 SP  
**Flavor**: *"The end is nigh."*

**Strategy**: Needs Yellow Common (Trash Can) or Blue Common (Necropolis) - both easily accessible brands. Decent difficulty, solid reward.

## 📊 STAGE STATISTICS

### By Difficulty (Appeal Required)
- **Easy** (8-10★): Face Face Face, Monster Ball, Filth Floor
- **Medium** (11-13★): Circus Berserkus, Anime Matsuri, Neon Nights, Witching Hour
- **Hard** (14-16★): Dungeon Drag, Apocalypse Now, Duality Ball, Royal Rumble
- **Expert** (20★): Grand Finale

### By SP Value
- **4 SP**: Face Face Face
- **5 SP**: Monster Ball, Filth Floor
- **6 SP**: Anime Matsuri, Circus Berserkus, Neon Nights
- **7 SP**: Witching Hour, Dungeon Drag, Apocalypse Now
- **8 SP**: Duality Ball, Royal Rumble
- **10 SP**: Grand Finale

### By Brand Accessibility
**Common Brand Requirements** (easiest):
- Face Face Face (any Face)
- Monster Ball (Blue Common)
- Filth Floor (any Trash)
- Apocalypse Now (Blue or Yellow Common)
- Circus Berserkus (Pink Common / Yellow Uncommon)

**Uncommon/Rare Requirements** (harder):
- Anime Matsuri (Pink Common/Uncommon)
- Neon Nights (Pink Uncommon)
- Dungeon Drag (Blue Uncommon / Purple Common)
- Witching Hour (Yellow/Blue Ultra Rare)
- Royal Rumble (Purple Ultra Rare)

**Special Requirements** (strategic):
- Grand Finale (Matching Look)
- Duality Ball (Fusion Look)

## 🎯 STRATEGIC INSIGHTS

### Early Game Targets (First 3-4 Slays)
1. **Face Face Face** - Quick 4 SP with low barrier
2. **Monster Ball** - Accessible with any Blue card
3. **Filth Floor** - If running Yellow/Trash build
4. **Anime Matsuri** - If Pink-focused

### Mid Game Targets (Rounds 4-8)
1. **Circus Berserkus** - Moderate difficulty, decent reward
2. **Apocalypse Now** - Common brands, 7 SP
3. **Neon Nights** - If invested in Hyper-Glitch
4. **Duality Ball** - If running Fusion strategy

### Late Game Targets (Final Slays)
1. **Grand Finale** - 10 SP jackpot for Matching Look
2. **Royal Rumble** - If you've secured Gilded Cage items
3. **Witching Hour** - Ultra Rare flex play
4. **Dungeon Drag** - Solid 7 SP closer

## 📝 DESIGN NOTES

### Balance Philosophy
Stages are designed with a risk/reward curve:
- Easy Stages (low SP) = Accessible prerequisites
- Hard Stages (high SP) = Specific brand requirements
- Special Stages (variable) = Strategic deck-building requirements

### Prerequisite Distribution
- **7 Stages** require specific brand items (58%)
- **2 Stages** require deck-building strategies (17%) - Matching/Fusion
- **2 Stages** are universal-ish (17%) - Face slot, Trash mechanic
- **1 Stage** requires two rare brands (8%) - Witching Hour

This ensures all deck archetypes have viable paths to victory while rewarding focused brand strategies.
