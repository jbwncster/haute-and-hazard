## ⚡ TURN PHASES (IN ORDER)

### 1️⃣ TRANSFORMATION
- Equip cards from hand to Player Mat slots
- Replace existing cards if desired
- Old cards → discard pile

### 2️⃣ THE REVEAL  
- Trigger card abilities ("On Play", "Set")
- Calculate total Appeal (★)
- Calculate total Tips ($)
- Check for Brand Matching bonuses

### 3️⃣ SHOPPING SPREE
- Spend Tips ($) to buy from Market Row
- Bought cards → discard pile
- Immediately replace cards in Market Row

### 4️⃣ SLAY OR BATTLE
- **Slay**: Meet Stage requirements → Claim Stage + SP
- **Battle**: Challenge opponent → Winner gains SP, Loser gets Extermination
- **Pass**: Do nothing

### 5️⃣ CLEANUP
- Discard all Accessories
- Discard all Action cards played
- Draw back to 5 cards
- End turn

## 🎨 PLAYER MAT SLOTS

**CORE SLOTS** (permanent until replaced):
- 💋 **Face** (Makeup)
- 💇 **Wig** (Hair)
- 👗 **Body** (Torso/Dress)
- 👠 **Shoes** (Footwear)

**TEMPORARY SLOT**:
- 💍 **Accessory** (discarded each turn)

---

## 🎯 BRAND MATCHING

### MATCHING LOOK (Full Set)
- All 4 core slots = SAME brand color
- **Bonus**: Appeal × 2
- Example: All Purple = 12★ becomes 24★

### FUSION LOOK (Mixed Set)
- Exactly 2 different brand colors in core slots
- **Bonus**: Trigger "Set" abilities on cards
- Example: 2 Pink + 2 Blue = Fusion

⚠️ **Accessories DON'T count for matching!**

---

## 💰 RESOURCES

### TIPS ($)
- Currency to buy cards
- Generated by Actions & some abilities
- Resets to 0 each turn

### APPEAL (★)
- Power to Slay Stages
- Generated by equipped fashion items
- Cumulative from all equipped cards

### STYLE POINTS (SP)
- Victory points
- Gained from Slaying Stages
- **Game ends at 30 SP** or when Stage deck empties

---

## ⚔️ COMBAT (BATTLES)

### Attack Calculation
- Your Attack = Appeal + Attack bonuses
- Your Defense = Base 0 + Defense bonuses

### Resolution
- Compare net damage: (Attack - Defense)
- Higher value wins
- Tie = no winner (both penalized)

### Rewards/Penalties
- **Winner**: +2 SP
- **Loser**: +1 Extermination card

---

## 🎴 CARD TYPES

### FASHION ITEMS
- Equipment for Player Mat slots
- Provide Appeal (★)
- Have special abilities
- Stay equipped until replaced

### ACTIONS
- Played from hand for immediate effect
- Usually generate Tips ($) or Appeal (★)
- Discarded after use

### STAGES
- Challenge cards to Slay
- Have prerequisites & target Appeal
- Award SP when claimed

### EXTERMINATIONS
- Penalty cards
- Clog your deck
- Reduce final SP

---

## 📚 KEY ABILITIES

**DRAW X** - Draw X cards immediately

**SYNERGY** - Bonus if condition met (e.g., "If you have Pink Wig...")

**DEFENSE** - Reduces damage in Battles

**ATTACK** - Increases damage or disrupts opponent

**WILD** - Counts as any color for Sets

**TRASH** - Remove from game permanently

**CHAIN** - Part of Vogue combo (Hands → Walk → Finish)

## 🏆 WINNING

### Game Ends When:
1. Stage deck depleted (all 12 Slayed)
2. Any player reaches 30 SP
3. Boutique deck runs out

### Final Score:
- Stage SP
- Collection bonus SP (special cards)
- Minus Extermination penalties

**Highest SP wins!**  
**Tiebreaker**: Most expensive collection (sum of card costs)

## 💡 QUICK TIPS

✅ **DO:**
- Build toward Matching or Fusion Looks for bonuses
- Replace weak starter cards early
- Consider defensive cards if battles are common
- Track your SP count

❌ **DON'T:**
- Forget to discard Accessories at turn end
- Buy cards you can't afford
- Overlook Stage prerequisites
- Ignore your Queen's passive ability
