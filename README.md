**Haute & Hazard** is a competitive drag fashion deck-building game for **2–5 players**, with an experimental **Solo Circuit v0.2** one-player mode in development.

Choose a Queen, build a wardrobe from a shared Market, assemble a Coordinate, earn Tips, build Appeal, Slay venue Stages, and challenge rivals in **Dragdagulan†** Lip Sync battles.

**Build your deck. Assemble your look. Slay the Stage.**

> **† Legal/IP review:** `Dragdagulan` is working prototype terminology credited to *Drag Den Philippines*. It remains subject to legal/IP, trademark, and publisher review before commercial release. See [Attributions](docs/ATTRIBUTIONS.md).

## Current development target: v7.9

**v7.9 is the current base-game rules/card target.**

The central v7.9 changes are:

- **Power-like Tips generated by playing cards during Transformation**;
- a printed Tip value on every Poker card, including 0;
- printed Tips generated once when each card is played from hand;
- purchased cards going to the **Backstage Archive** before entering a later draw cycle;
- a fully unique **144-card Wardrobe**:
  - 130 unique Fashion cards;
  - 14 unique Actions;
- Fashion may be played for Tips even when it is not equipped, preventing dead resource draws.

The base Poker Deck remains **240 cards**:

- 60 starter cards;
- 144 Wardrobe cards;
- 16 Thrift Store Throwbacks;
- 20 Penalty cards.

Stages, Queens, Player Aids, rules, and optional Solo cards are separate components.

## v7.9 turn order

**1. Transformation → 2. Reveal → 3. Shopping → 4. Slay / Dragdagulan† / Pass → 5. Cleanup**

During Transformation, play cards from your 5-card hand one at a time to generate their printed Tips. Unplayed cards generate nothing, already-equipped Masters do not generate Tips again, and Fashion may be played for Tips without being equipped. Spend Tips from a five-card Wardrobe Rack; purchases go to Backstage Archive. At Cleanup, refill the Rack, archive the rest of your hand/played non-Masters, and draw 5.

## Solo Circuit v0.2

Solo Circuit is a separate experimental module for v7.9.

It uses **30 solo cards**:

- 18 House Queen Automa cards;
- 12 House Queen Personality cards.

v0.2 adds:

- a **four-round Stage timer**;
- explicit House Base Appeal:
  - `max(0, Stage Slay Target - 6)`;
- explicit House Stage Appeal and scoring;
- v7.9 play-generated Tip compatibility.

Start with [Solo Circuit v0.2 Rules](docs/SOLO_CIRCUIT_AUTOMA_PLAYTEST.md) or the [Solo v0.2 Release Index](releases/solo-circuit-v0.2/README.md).
