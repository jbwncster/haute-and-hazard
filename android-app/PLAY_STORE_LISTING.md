## App name
Haute & Hazard

## Short description
Build bold drag looks, shop the Wardrobe, and slay the Stage in a solo card game.

## Full description
Step into the spotlight in Haute & Hazard, the digital playtest adaptation of the tabletop drag-fashion deck-building game.

Choose a Queen, build a persistent fashion Coordinate, play cards for Tips, shop a shared Wardrobe Rack, and race the House Queen across a dramatic Solo Circuit. Match Tenets and Brands, chase Perfect Illusion multipliers, Slay themed Stages, and survive Dragdagulan lip-sync battles.

This v0.1 Android release is built from the current Haute & Hazard v7.9 game data and Solo Circuit v0.2 playtest system.

Features:
• 12 playable Queens
• 12 themed Stages
• 144-card Wardrobe plus starter and supporting card pools
• Persistent Face, Wig, Body, Shoes, and Accessory slots
• Tips, Appeal, Matching, Perfect Illusion, and Stage scoring
• Solo House Queen Automa
• 3-, 5-, or 12-Stage run options
• Offline play
• Local save and resume

Haute & Hazard is an original independent game currently in active playtesting. Rules, balance, terminology, and content may change between versions.
